# Guns and melee

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-03. Grep a heading, then read that part: don't read the whole file.

### WeaponMechanics
- Ammo can be items, XP or money. We use item ammo in the locked upper inventory. XP ammo is out because the XP bar shows the bag.
- It takes ammo from the inventory itself; the lock only blocks players moving items.
- It can use the drop and swap-hand keys as gun controls, so the inventory lock must run after WeaponMechanics sees those keys. The swap-hand key must never move the bag out of the offhand.
- It downloads its own resource pack; turn off its pack sending.

### WeaponMechanics (verified in its 4.3.1 source and with bots)
- It reads Q (PlayerDropItemEvent) and F (PlayerSwapHandItemsEvent) at LOW with ignoreCancelled=true, schedules the trigger for the next tick, and never re-checks. Cancelling at HIGH/HIGHEST is safe; cancelling at LOWEST/LOW breaks reload and firemode.
- Players need `weaponmechanics.use.<weapon>` (or `.*`) or guns won't shoot or reload.
- No weapon may have a `Dual_Wielding` rule (removed from every weapon file 2026-09-25): WeaponMechanics counts ANY item in the offhand as dual wielding, and its default guns had `Circumstance: Dual_Wielding: DENY` on shooting (the .50 GS also on scoping, plus +50% spread), so with the bag in the offhand no gun fired; grenades worked because they have no such rule. Found by the owner; `bots\run.js wm-ammo` now checks every sold gun fires and reloads with the bag.
- Every weapon we sell needs `Info.Cancel.Drop_Item: true` and `Info.Cancel.Swap_Hands: true` (without Swap_Hands, pressing F cancels a running reload even though Skript cancels the swap).
- Default guns have no `Reload.Ammo` section, so they reload for free. Item ammo needs `Reload.Ammo.Ammos` per gun plus an ammo type in `ammos\*.yml`.
- Its skin selector wipes every custom_model_data flag on every trigger (2026-10-13, bytecode: RelativeSkinSelector.apply always calls `setCustomModelData(Integer)` then setItemMeta; SkinHandler.tryUse runs on each right-click packet, also every 4 ticks while fire is held). So DonatingPhone puts its flags back every tick (the clock flags in flagClock, the firing flag in firingTick) before the tick's slot packets go out, and the client never sees the gap; an RCON read between a click packet and the tick can (tests read the client's set_slot copies for flags during a held trigger: gunfx.js).
- The Shotgun (R9_0) loads one shell per 4-tick reload since 2026-10-13 (from empty 14 shells = 2.8 s, then WeaponMechanics' pump, 15 ticks; a shot stops the loading and fires). Until then it loaded the whole tube in one 4-tick reload (0.2 s with rounds left, 0.9 s from empty), ever since item ammo came in (2026-09-25): its `Ammo_Per_Reload: 1` sat right under `Ammo:` and was never loaded (the next note). Each shell is its own reload (its own events; ammo-per-reload 1 in the reload event); WeaponMechanics chains them in the same tick (ReloadHandler: the task's run calls finishReload, then starts the next load), stops at a full tube or no spare shells, and from empty works the pump right after the last shell (the next load's event is cancelled as full and the pump OPEN closes it). If the spare shells run out first, the pump stays open and the next click works it instead of shooting. A shot mid-load: ShootHandler stops the reload and fires (from empty with the pump open: it works the pump instead).
- MechanicsCore 4.3.1's config loader (FileReader.fillOneFile, source) skips every key whose full path starts with the TEXT of a key a serializer took before it, with no '.' boundary: `R9_0.Reload.Ammo_Per_Reload` came after the serialized `R9_0.Reload.Ammo` and was silently dropped (no warning), so `config.getInt("R9_0.Reload.Ammo_Per_Reload", -1)` read -1. Keys read raw at runtime must come before any sibling whose name is their prefix (`Ammo_Per_Reload` above `Ammo:`; checked over every sold weapon file 2026-10-13: the other prefix pairs, like Recoil's `Damping`/`Damping_Recovery`, sit inside sections their serializer reads itself).
- WeaponMechanics picks a click's skin before the shot stops a reload (4.3.1: the trigger's skin update runs first), so a gun shot mid-reload kept its Reload state (+3000) until its next skin update; GunFx asks WeaponMechanics for a fresh skin (SkinHandler.tryUse) when a reload is cancelled with the same gun still in hand. At a reload from empty a pump gun builds its Open and Close tasks (firing their WeaponFirearmEvents) even when it won't run them (Ammo_Per_Reload set): GunFx ignores a pump event in the tick its reload clock started. Named skins (a Skin key that isn't an action, `ADD 10000`..`90000`) are added to the number by WeaponMechanics itself and a plugin picks one with WeaponSkinEvent#setSkin: unlike a custom_model_data flag it survives WeaponMechanics rewriting the item (ShellLoad uses Shell_In/Shell_Out).
- It takes ammo from slots 0-35 except the held slot, editing stacks in place. Never put ammo in the hotbar.
- Never use `Ammo_Switch_Trigger`, `Unload_Ammo_On_Reload: true`, `Weapon_Converter_Check` or `Ammo_Converter_Check`: they add items with addItem (first free hotbar slot) or rewrite items in place (could turn the bag into a weapon).
- Give guns with amount 1 into a fixed slot: `wm give <player> <weapon> 1 {slot:N}` (N = raw slot, 0-8 hotbar). A stack of 2+ guns drops the extras on every shot. `{ammo:N}` sets the rounds loaded.
- Default grenades use placeable materials (RED_CANDLE, TNT, BEACON...). inventory.sk cancels block placing, so that's covered.
- Its `Weapon_Info_Display.Action_Bar` fights hud.sk's action bar; turn it off or route ammo info elsewhere when building hud.sk. Keep `Show_Ammo_In.Exp_*` off: hud.sk draws the XP bar (magazine + spare rounds, which WM can't show).
- WM keys (PDC): `weaponmechanics:weapon-title`, `weaponmechanics:ammo-left`, `weaponmechanics:ammo-name`.
- Item ammo (verified 2026-09-25, bots\run.js wm-ammo): WeaponMechanics matches ammo items only by the PDC string `weaponmechanics:ammo-name` = the ammo's title, so Skript builds them (SkBee: `set string tag "PublicBukkitValues;weaponmechanics:ammo-name" of custom nbt of {_i}`; ";" is SkBee's path separator). It takes ammo at the END of a reload from slots 0-35 except the held slot, from several stacks, and ignores other types. Without `Reload.Ammo` a gun reloads for free (every installed gun did).
- `wm give <p> <W> 1 {slot:N}` gives a gun with a FULL magazine; shop.sk always adds `ammo:0`, or taking a gun out and back in would be free ammo. A consumable stack is given with `wm give <p> Stim <total> {slot:N}` (safe because Stim has `Consume_Item_On_Shoot`: no "drop the extras").
- Bullets (owner, 2026-09-26: "look like actual bullets, or at least bullet streaks like other games, and not snowballs"): every sold gun's `Projectile:` is one of ours in `projectiles\Donating_Projectiles.yml` (donating_pistol_bullet .50 GS, donating_smg_bullet Uzi, donating_rifle_bullet AK-47, donating_pellet R9-0), each with exactly the physics of the WeaponMechanics default it replaced, but `Type: ITEM_DISPLAY` with `Projectile_Item_Or_Block: {Type: IRON_NUGGET, Custom_Model_Data: 7001|7002|7003}` (light, rifle, pellet). That's a fake entity (packets only, MechanicsCore's FakeEntity) that WeaponMechanics turns along its flight every tick (yaw = atan2(−x, z), pitch = atan(−y / horizontal), checked in AProjectile); the pack's `iron_nugget.json` (range_dispatch on the custom model data) draws a copper bullet with a glowing tracer streak, nose along −z (the 26.3 client turns a fixed display by rotationYXZ(−yaw, pitch, 0), then the item by 180° around Y). Custom model data, not `Item_Model`: an item_model the client doesn't have (no pack, or a failed download) is a 1-block magenta and black cube; this way it's a small iron nugget. For its first tick a bullet sits at the shooter's eye (WeaponMechanics only moves the display from the second tick), so the model is drawn ahead of the true position (z = −16 to 12, the last 0.25 blocks inside the head) and has no backward (south) faces: nothing sticks out of the shooter's head, and the shooter sees nothing on that frame. The display then jumps a tick of flight at a time (Projectile_Speed / 20 blocks: 4 at 80, no interpolation), so it reads as fast dashes (the streak is ~1.75 blocks). Free WeaponMechanics 4.3.1 has no particle trails: the `Trail:` blocks in its weapon files are for the paid WeaponMechanicsCosmetics and do nothing. Model elements use `light_emission: 15` (26.3 has it), so tracers glow at night. The hit box stays WeaponMechanics' default Size 0.1. `bots\run.js wm-ammo` checks every gun's bullets arrive as item displays with the right custom model data, facing within 6° of their flight, and the models' shape. A new gun needs one of these projectiles (or a new one like them).
- Damage (owner, 2026-09-26: "the strongest gun kills someone without armor in 4 shots (except snipers, make them 2) and someone in full strongest armor in 10-12"): WeaponMechanics' own damage skips vanilla armor (DamageUtil builds the event with only the base damage; javap-checked); a hit does D = base × (1 + Per_Armor_Point × armor points + hit spot + movement), clamped to 0-300%. config.yml `Per_Armor_Point: -6%` (was -3%), so the best gear (Tactical Helmet 3 + Heavy Vest 8 = 11 points) takes 66% off. The hit-spot modifiers add to that, so they're small now (review fix: feet were -50%, 0 damage through the best gear): Head +15%, Body/Arms/Legs 0, Feet -5%, Back +5%, and `Min: 20%`. Base damage a body shot: AK-47 5.5 (4 shots unarmored anywhere; in the best gear body/arms/legs 11, feet 13, back 10, head 8), the guns added 2026-10-06: Old Revolver 5.3 (4 shots; best gear 1.80, 12), Brave Patriot 5.1 (4; 12), Combat Rifle 4.9 a round in 3-round bursts (5; a burst never kills: 14.7), Sniper Rifle 12 flat (2 shots, never 1: a head shot 13.8; best gear 4.08, 5), Uzi 4.85, .50 GS 4.3, R9-0 0.6 a pellet (a point-blank blast of 10 pellets = 6, so 4 blasts), Combat Knife 5 (its own head and backstab bonuses are 0: they stacked with the global modifiers into 2-hit kills); every dropoff scaled the same. A sniper, if one is ever sold: d ≥ 10 and < 20 (2 shots unarmored, never 1). Verified with bots (bots\run.js wm-damage, 6 checks: the drops 5.5, a blast of 3-6 at 4 blocks, 5 a knife hit, 1.87 armored = 11 shots, a head shot through the best gear 2.70, leg shots 1.87). Bots can't be hurt for about 6 s after joining, and a gun still fires bullets in flight after a series (the target has health boost in the test).
- Not sold (v1): explosives (Grenade, Semtex, Cluster break blocks: `Block_Damage BREAK`, could open vault walls past traps; Flashbang uses potion effects that also hit passive players and safe zones, which pvp.sk only protects from damage), Airstrike, Sky_Torch, RPG_7, Fatman. - **GunFx** (DonatingPhone `GunFx.java`, config `gunfx.*`): each gun gets a `use_cooldown` component with its own group `donating:gun/<title>` (on WeaponGenerateEvent, and on any gun held or in the hotbar at join without it) and `swing_animation none`; it starts that group's cooldown (Player#setCooldown) when WeaponMechanics reloads (as long as the reload: max(complete, reload time); a shell-at-a-time gun its one load), after a shot (`shot`: Classic Pistol 6 ticks, the slide; Shotgun 15, the kick and the pump) and on equip (`draw`: Machine Gun 20, AK-48 30), and ends it on a reload's cancel or end. `alone` (Shotgun 5): a shot that works no firearm action plays only its kick (WeaponMechanics 4.3.1: the shot event comes after the round is used; the action runs when the state isn't READY, or with rounds left that are a multiple of Firearm_Action_Frequency); each player's clocks are numbered so a cut never ends a later clock (an empty gun's reload starts in the same tick as its last shot).
