# Navigation and the GPS

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-03. Grep a heading, then read that part: don't read the whole file.

### Navigation: no minimap mod (decided 2026-09-24)
- GTA and Jailbreak have a corner minimap, which needs a client mod. Shader-based "vanilla minimap" plugins break on client updates (NMinimap fails on 26.3 and takes the whole server pack down with it), so Donating uses two vanilla features instead:
  - Locator bar (the 1.21.6+ bar above the hotbar) as a compass: POI dots (bank, shops, base, open heists, later your car or a GPS target), colored per type, bigger when closer.
  - The phone (hotbar 9) is the city GPS map: hold it for a GPS view that follows you, right-click for the whole city, with POI labels and yourself (see Phone plugin).
- Passive players show up on everyone's map (green arrow; their name shows when you hover it on the big map) and as a green dot on everyone's locator bar. Non-passive players never show on other players' maps or locator bars (they still see themselves). Turning passive on tells the player they're visible now; turning it off tells them they're hidden again.
- Custom phone plugin: APPROVED by the owner (2026-09-24), the one exception to "no Java plugins". The owner's vision:
  - The phone in hand is a player-centered map (the screen follows you, like a phone GPS).
  - Right-click the phone: the full view of the city map.
  - Buttons or side buttons around the map (resource pack or otherwise), so the phone does its other jobs too (stats, passive, garage, bounties, help), not only the map.
  - Passive players: a green arrow with a green name on the map; non-passive players don't show on other players' maps. (2026-09-25: the name only while hovering the arrow on the big map, and the big map zooms out to fit a big city, with arrows that shrink with it.)
  Keep the plugin small and driven from Skript (commands, scoreboard tags or PDC), with the game rules in Skript. Built 2026-09-25: see "Phone plugin (DonatingPhone)" below.
- Inside heists the XP fill bar (now the ammo bar, hud.sk) needs zero waypoints for that player. Hiding POI entities isn't enough (passive players are waypoints too, and hiding a player hides their body). Instead set the robber's own `waypoint_receive_range` base to 0 on heist entry and back to 6e7 on exit, death and join (verified: ServerPlayer.onAttributeUpdated re-adds the player to the waypoint manager at once). That also takes a passive robber off everyone's bar while inside. An exit/base waypoint and the fill bar can't show together; only the level number (rounds left) shows in both. Outside heists the locator bar shows instead of the ammo bar except for 5 s after each change (so while you shoot).
- Points of interest on the locator bar since 2026-11-05 (nav.sk + DonatingPhone's Waypoints.java): every POI, open heist and quest giver is a waypoint the plugin sends straight to each client (`/dphone way set <id> <world> <x> <y> <z> <color> <style> | remove <id> | list`; nav.sk's every-10-s pass calls it only when something changed, `{-poi::state::<id>}`). No stands and no forced chunks: a forced chunk keeps every chunk within 13 of it in memory once visited (docs/performance.md). The client works out direction and distance from the position, so nothing is sent while players move; the far icon is still the small dot, from the style's distances. Robbers in a heist: the plugin reads `waypoint_receive_range` every second and takes them all off the bar at 0. At the first load `{poimigrated}` runs `forceload remove all` once and forgets `{poichunk::*}`, `{poichunkw::*}`, `{poiat::*}`; the plugin removes old `donating_poi` stands as their chunks load. Needs PacketEvents (already required by WeaponMechanics). Bot test: `poi`.
- Before that (the rules still apply, the stands don't): staff `/dpoi add <base|shop|spawn|landmark> [name]` where they stand (`remove <n>`, `list`, `dump`), and every heist while it's open or running (at its hologram, else the region's centre). Each is an invisible marker armor stand (tags `donating_poi`, `poi_<n>` / `poi_h_<heist>`) with `waypoint_transmit_range` 6e7 (0 hides it: a heist cooling down or disabled), colored with `waypoint modify ... color` (core.sk `poi::<type>::color`: base aqua, shop yellow, spawn white, landmark light_purple; heists `difficulty::<d>::waypoint`: green, yellow, gold, dark_red). Their chunks are force-loaded; every 10 s each POI gets exactly one stand (a missing one is summoned, extras and stands nothing refers to are killed). Summoned by the console, so POIs live in the main world. Saved: `{poi::<n>}` = "type|world|x|y|z|name", the counter is `{poicounter}` (outside the list: a counter inside `{poi::*}` was read as a POI). Robbers in a heist still receive no waypoints. Review fixes (2026-09-26): each POI remembers where its stand is (`{poiat::<id>}`) and the chunk it force-loaded (`{poichunk::<id>}` = "cx:cz"); a POI that moves (a heist's hologram set again) kills its old stand first and summons the new one on the next pass; a new stand always gets its range and color (the `{-poi::state}` cache is reset); a chunk no POI uses any more is released (`forceload remove`); `/dpoi add` refuses other worlds and a heist outside the main world gets no stand (the console's summon always lands in the main world). Verified with bots (bots\run.js poi, 12 checks: the stand, its range, a track packet with that stand's UUID in the shop color, a missing stand coming back as a waypoint again, heists only while open, a disabled heist's stand in its difficulty's color, a moved hologram's POI, remove, staff only).
- Quest givers (quests.sk) are blue dots too (see "Quest givers").
- District names (districts.sk, 2026-09-29): a WorldGuard region `district_<id>` is a district (`passthrough allow`, like every Donating region); entering it (walking, driving, a teleport) shows "— Old Town —" on the action bar (hudBar priority 1), once per district every `district::repeat` (30 s); the main sidebar's "Area" line (%donating_district%) is the smallest district you're in, else "the outskirts". Its name: `/ddistrict name <id> <name...>` (`{districtname::<id>}`; `default` clears it), else the id made readable ("old_town" -> "Old Town"). Staff: `/ddistrict name|list|here|dump` (the name takes the id or the region's full id and warns when no such region exists yet; tab completion, /adminhelp). The name shows at once on a region enter and also from the every-second pass when the district changed (review fix, 2026-09-29: back out of an inner district, across an overlap, as a car passenger and on join no enter fires). A district region must reach from under the streets to above the roofs (`//expand vert` before `/rg define`). bots\run.js districts (11 checks).
- Other orientation ideas (not built): street signs. (POI icons on the phone map: built 2026-09-28; a big wall map: built 2026-09-29, see Phone plugin.)

### GPS (gps.sk + DonatingPhone, built 2026-09-26)
- Owner, 2026-09-26: waypoints you set on the minimap or that quests set automatically, shown on the minimap, with an arrow or highlight in the game that goes away within the waypoint's area, and it has to follow the roads and cope with a player taking another way. Designed, critiqued (3 critics + a synthesis: the flow field, a ring instead of a beam, slots, the car key as a map) and reviewed before merging.
- The way (plugin, `Gps.java`, `FlowField.java`, `Roads.java`): a **flow field**, like water running downhill to a drain: one Dijkstra outward from the target gives every cell of the city grid its next step, so from wherever the player is the best way is already known. Taking another street, or a teleport, is a new route at once with no rerouting (and no flicker). A target can be several spots at once (every chop shop, every base): the search starts from all of them, so the nearest along the roads wins. Costs per step onto a cell (tenths, config `gps.foot-cost` / `gps.car-cost`): road 1 / 1, open ground 1.5 / 6, blocked 15 / 40; the profile is the car's while the player is in any vehicle (walkers cut through an alley, cars keep to the roads); a diagonal step pays for the two cells beside it (no cutting a building's corner). A field is 30-60 ms at the 512x512-cell cap on a worker thread (unit-tested in the scratchpad's FlowTest), cached per target and profile (12 kept), rebuilt only when the target, profile or grid changes.
- The road grid (`/dphone roads scan [x1 z1 x2 z2]`, staff/op, run on the local server; saved as `plugins/DonatingPhone/roads.bin`, upload it with the world): one cell per city-map pixel (grouped k x k over `gps.max-cells` 262,144). A column's ground is the highest spot between `gps.street-y-min` and `street-y-max` (default the whole world: once the city exists set them to a few blocks below the street and the street level + 2, so buildings and walls taller than a curb read as blocked; wall, fence, gate, pane and bar tops never count as ground) where a solid block has two non-solid blocks above: ROAD if it's one of `gps.road-blocks` (default black/gray concrete and powder, white and yellow concrete), OPEN otherwise, BLOCKED under water or lava or with no room to stand. A cell is ROAD if any column is (streets never break), else BLOCKED if at least half are (thin walls stay), else OPEN. The scan loads at most 1 chunk per tick, 4 at a time, pauses while ticks run over 40 ms, never generates land (a missing chunk is open ground), reads snapshots without light on its own thread, and swaps the finished grid in at once. `/dphone roads info` (counts, what it was scanned with), `cancel`, `show <player> [on|off]` (road cells yellow, blocked red on that player's big map). Before a scan, outside the city or in another world the GPS points straight at the target ("direct").
- Targets (gps.sk): slots **pin** (a place the player picked: a left-click on the big map, or `/gps` / the phone's GPS app: its Quests, Heists, Shops and Places pages), **quest** (contracts.sk's `ctGps`: the car's spot, then every chop shop while driving it, or back to the car when out of it) and **loot** (every base POI while carrying loot outside a heist). The newest objective wins (like GTA): a new pin puts the quest on hold; the quest's next objective (find -> deliver) puts the pin on hold; `/gps` switches back (the one on hold is listed; right-click removes a pin). With neither, the loot target. All slots show as icons on the phone map (pin a red X, quest a target mark, base a white X; names on the big map); only the active one gets a route. gps.sk sends each slot only when it changes (`dphone gps <p> set <slot> <world> <radius> <x,y,z[;...]> <label>`, `clear <slot>`, `active <slot|none>`).
- Shown to that player only (plugin): the route on the phone (2 px with a black edge on the held phone, 1 px on the big map; redrawn on the held phone's re-centers), the name and distance in the held phone's strip ("Chop shop 340m  R:map"), and in the world a dust trail on the way ahead (24-64 blocks, longer the faster you go, along the way's straight stretches, force-sent so Minimal particles and distance don't hide it), a ring of dust on the ground around the target's area within 48 blocks, and a dot on the locator bar at the next corner (16-40 blocks ahead): an invisible marker stand made invisible to everyone else before it exists (`setVisibleByDefault(false)`, then `showEntity`, then the color, then the waypoint range; tag `donating_gps`, colors `gps.color.*`: pin #D040E0, quest #FF5A1F, loot #30C8C0, apart from the POI and heist dots). The boss bar (hud.sk: heist > cops > contract > GPS) reads the plugin's metadata: "GPS · Gun Shop ↗ 320m", "↱ 40m · 320m" with a turn coming up.
- The trail's white orbs (2026-11-05, `orb()` in Gps.java): an END_ROD on every second dust. Dust is lit by what's around it, so at night and indoors the quest's orange goes brown, and on the prison's orange floor (the first trail a new player has to follow) it was a few dark specks. Compared side by side on that floor at midnight: dust, TRAIL sparks (even pale ones: a pixel), wax_on and small_flame all lost clearly to end_rod. It's white on every kind of way: the dust keeps the color. The ring still has dust and coloured TRAIL sparks. An orb lives about 3 s and falls to the floor, so a few linger behind you.
- The turn text (2026-11-05): the angle at a corner uses the way in along the route (`corner()`: 6 blocks back along the cells; `lineCorner()`: the path's points 2 or more blocks back), not the line from the player: being off-centre in a wide corridor isn't a turn. At a staff path's first point there's nothing behind it, so there it uses the player's own line, only from 6 or more blocks away.
- Shop missions (story.sk `storyGpsSpots`, "poi:shop:", 2026-11-05): the keeper at the counter whose `{shop::keepers::*}` title has the word (same world), else the POIs. Before, the GPS said "here" at the door with the keeper 15 m inside. Staff paths already go through the door.
- Arriving (plugin): the flat distance to the nearest spot within its radius (a pin 8, `gps::pin-radius`, 14 in a car, at any height; a quest or the base within 12 blocks of height, at their own radius), also when the last 5-tick move passed through it (a car covers 6 blocks; only a real move: the same world, not dead or paused before, at most 20 blocks, so a respawn or teleport across a pin doesn't count), latched 1.5 s so gps.sk's once-a-second check sees it, and not un-arrived until a bit outside it. Inside the area the trail, ring and dot go (the owner's "goes away when you are within the area"); gps.sk clears an arrived pin ("Arrived · Gun Shop" on the action bar, a chime). Messages go through hudBar; the only chat line is the big map's one-time tip (data `gps-tip`).
- Inside a heist gps.sk sets the tag `donating_gps_paused` (every second): no trail, ring, dot or arrival (robbers receive no waypoints there anyway).
- The big map: left-click (the plugin cancels the click at LOWEST: with the camera tilted it's a LEFT_CLICK_BLOCK on the ground, and a click on an entity is a PrePlayerAttackEntityEvent) runs the console command `gpspick <player> <x> <z> <reach>`; gps.sk drops a pin there at the player's own height (reading the ground could load land anywhere), snapped to a heist's exit spot on a heist building or to a POI or heist within reach (with its name; "Pin" otherwise), or removes the pin under the cursor. The bottom strip says "L-click: set a pin" / "remove pin". No camera tilt while in a vehicle until 2026-10-21 (drivers couldn't open the big map, "Stop the car"); since then drivers open it with the key's right-click, tilted like passengers, and the held phone's GPS view works while driving: the car key (garage.sk `carKey(p, plate)`) is a filled map with the player's phone map id and custom model data `donating:carkey` (the pack draws it as the key).
- Metadata (only DonatingPhone writes it; up to 5 ticks old): `donating_gps_next` (Location), `donating_gps_left` (Double), `donating_gps_state` (route | direct | arrived | paused), `donating_gps_turn` (left | right | ""), `donating_gps_turn_in` (Double). `/dphone gps <player>` prints the state for tests; `/dphone status <player>` adds the route colors' palette bytes.
- Numbers decided (the owner left them to Claude, 2026-09-26): the colors (pin purple #D040E0: no Minecraft map color is purple, so the route stands out on the phone, where blue would sink into water; jobs orange #FF5A1F; the base teal #30C8C0), the costs above, and the arrival areas (a pin 8 blocks, 14 in a car; the base 10; a chop shop 6).
- Verified with bots (bots\run.js gps: PLAYTEST 114), unit runs of the flow field (U road, alley, corner, two targets, a 512x512 city in ~50 ms) and in the real client (PLAYTEST 115). Reviewed (3 reviewers + a skeptic, 2026-09-26): 12 confirmed, all fixed (teleports counting as passing through, rooftop pins, wall and fence tops as ground, the scan box clamped to the city, a reload cancelling a scan, flow fields keeping their cost arrays, the moving-car target with no chop shop, the key's strip saying R:map, pins on heist buildings, picks loading chunks, the contract bar during a pin, passengers without the tilt).
- GPS paths (DonatingPhone `Paths.java`, 2026-10-14; the owner, playtesting the prison: "have a way to set paths that the GPS follows (so then it doesnt go through walls)"): the road grid is one flat layer of one city world, so in the prison (a basement, floors, fences) the GPS pointed straight through walls. Staff lay a graph per world: `/dphone path add [name] [--nolink]` at your feet (linked to the last node you added), `addat <world> <x> <y> <z> [id=<n>] [nolink] [name]` (console), `link|unlink <a> <b>`, `remove <n>` (an id that isn't there: "no node #<id>"), `list [world]`, `near` (the 6 nearest, seen or hidden), `show [on|off]` (particles for you: green nodes, red ones with no link, yellow links), `clear <world> confirm`, `dump [world]` (clear + addat with ids + link lines, also in `plugins/DonatingPhone/paths-dump.txt`), `info`; tab completion. Saved in `plugins/DonatingPhone/paths.yml` (at most once a second; loaded once at start, not by /dphone reload). Ids are global (the test uses 9001-9008). In a world with a graph Gps asks Paths first: like the FlowField, one Dijkstra outward from the target (seeded from every link within `paths.range` 24 of each spot, the closest point on it, preferring links the target can see; none: the graph's nearest node) gives every node its next node; the player joins on the link, lone node or spot that makes the whole trip shortest (off the graph counts `paths.off-cost` 1.5x a block, height `paths.vertical-weight` 3x so another floor isn't near), the best one their eyes can see (rays to its spot + 1.2, at most 8 a pass; none seen: the best link, never a spot through a wall), kept until another is `paths.hysteresis` 2 blocks better; a player on the target's own link goes straight along it. No link within range of the player: the road grid or the straight line, as before. Outputs follow the polyline (`t.line`): the dot at the farthest point in a straight line (3D, within 1 block) at the path's height + 1, the turn there "up"/"down" (the next stretch climbs or drops 2.5+ blocks: gps.sk says "go up in 6m"), else left/right; the trail at the path's height + 0.25; the ring at the target's height (any world with paths); the route on the phone from the player through the points; a quest's arrival height `paths.arrive-height` 4 (12 elsewhere; pins any height as before). `/dphone gps <player>` adds `way=path|roads|direct pick= line= via=<node ids> first=`. The prison's first graph: `tools\node\node.exe tools\make-jail-gps-paths.js [--check]` walks the build (the escape checker's walk model, copied: strict moves, drops of 3 at most, costs so jumps are only used when they save walking) with every door and breach open between the build's named points (the B1 cells and course, Hall B and A, the yard loop and across, the walk, the police station, the Gun Shop, the punch box and parkour, the lot, the gatehouse booth and console, the front gate and out), string-pulls each walk into corners joined by straight lines over walked floor (0.3 to each side too), splits links at T-junctions, and writes `tools/jail-gps-paths.txt` (187 nodes, 193 links; 197 nodes, 204 links since 2026-10-20; 203 nodes, 210 links since 2026-10-21; 210 nodes, 217 links since 2026-10-22, the lot's ladder walk last (nodes 209-215); a node keeping the id its spot had (the dugout, the punch box moved): walks to Sparks' three weapon part spots, added last so the older nodes keep their ids; the gym's ends in front of the dumbbell rack, as spotNear picks a point's top and no walk jumps onto it; `--check` tests every node's blocks against the world over RCON: all matched 2026-10-14; it reports a node in an unloaded chunk as different). Load it with its lines (RCON locally; on Minehut the console or the paths.yml it makes); run it again after the prison's build changes. Checked offline (a harness with rays against the build): nine prisoner trips route, crossing only closed doors and gates. bots\run.js gpspaths (20 checks, with the zoom and the per-world city). The wand (DonatingPhone `PathWand.java`, 2026-10-21; the owner: "an easy in-game way to add/move/remove/link nodes (e.g. a staff wand tool + a click menu)"; untested (cloud)): `/dphone path wand` puts a glinting blaze rod "GPS Path Wand" in an empty main hand (creative only); while it's in hand the paths show (like `show`) and the selected node has an aqua ring; right-click a block: a node on top of it (the block's collision top: a slab, a stair; a plant or carpet: the floor) linked to the selected one and selected, right-click the air: at your feet; left-click a node (the one nearest your aim, up to 48 blocks): select it; sneak + left-click a node: link it to the selected one (again: unlink); sneak + right-click: the selected node's menu (1 row: its info, Move it to your feet, Link to the nearest unlinked node within 32, Unlink all, Delete (a second click within 4 s), Deselect, Close); the wand never breaks or uses a block. New subcommands: `move <id>` (to your feet), `name <id> [name]`, `select [id]` (the next `add` links to it too); `add` selects the new node. Only donating.phone.admin. bots\run.js gpspaths has 4 wand checks.

### Personal views (pv.sk + DonatingPhone, built 2026-09-27)
- An entity only one player sees (like an AR sticker): pv.sk spawns a display with the tags donating_pv and pvo_<uuid> inside its spawn section; DonatingPhone's EntitySpawnEvent listener (LOWEST) sets it invisible to everyone before it's in the world; pv.sk reveals it to its owner. If anyone else can see it, it's removed and personal views switch off (`{-pv::broken}`, `/dpv reset`). Displays only, never persistent; NPCs use Citizens' playerfilter instead. /dphone pv <uuid> shows who it's sent to (tests). Verified with bots (bots\run.js pv, 7 checks: another player's client never gets the spawn packet).
- DonatingPhone `/dphone mark <player> <entity-uuid> <color> <range> | off | status`: a locator dot on an entity for one player (a hidden marker stand, the GPS dot's recipe), following the entity; the stand stays up to 40 ticks while the entity is missing (Citizens swaps a PLAYER NPC's entity when its skin loads).

### Navigation (verified 2026-09-24 in the Paper 1.21.11 jar with javap, with bots, and in the 26.3 client)
- Maps: every player carrying a copy of the same map id (anywhere in the inventory) is a white arrow on everyone's copy. `MapItemSavedData.tickCarriedBy` adds all carriers, then removes other carriers wearing an item from `#minecraft:map_invisibility_equipment` in an armor slot (hands don't count), but never the viewer itself. `ServerPlayer.doTick` runs that pass right before sending that player's map packet, so the hiding is per viewer and never flickers (12/12 packets in the bot test). Vanilla's tag only holds carved_pumpkin. (Before the phone plugin a datapack added structure_void as a boots-slot "map cloak"; gone now that every player has their own map id, and nav.sk takes old cloaks off.)
- Player arrows never get a name (the server passes null). Item `map_decorations` entries are copied into the shared map data once (null name, never moved or removed until a restart). Named banners clicked with a map become labeled markers for everyone (saved with the map). That's why inventory.sk locks banner right-clicks; staff with bypass can still add labels with a plain copy of the map (not the phone).
- Paper also drops a player's arrow for a viewer who can't see that player (`Player#canSee`), but that hides the player's body too; don't use it for maps.
- The phone's `minecraft:map_id` is set with SkBee: `set int tag "minecraft:map_id" of nbt of {_i} to N`. A map_id with no data file shows an empty map. Locked maps (cartography table + glass pane) keep their pixels but still show arrows, icons and labels.
- Map files: `world\data\minecraft\maps\<id>.dat` since 26.1 (before: `world\data\map_<id>.dat`; gzip NBT; fields at their default, like `scale: 0`, are left out). `world\data\minecraft\maps\last_id.dat` holds the last id (before: `idcounts.dat`). SkBee's structures moved to `world\generated\donating\structure\` (singular) and players to `world\players\data\`.
- Locator bar (icons since 2026-10-03: see "Locator-bar icons" in Phone plugin): any living entity (armor stands too) with `waypoint_transmit_range` > 0 is a waypoint for players within min(transmit, receive range). Players transmit 6e7 by default, so vanilla shows every player to everyone; nav.sk sets 0 unless passive. `waypoint modify <entity> color <color>` sets the dot color; custom icons need a resource pack (`waypoint_style`), untested. A waypoint entity is only tracked while its chunk is loaded (why POIs are packets now, not entities). Paper checks `CraftPlayer#canSee`, so Skript's `hide <entity> from <player>` removes one player's dot only.
- XP bar vs locator bar: while a player has any waypoint, the locator bar replaces the XP fill bar (the level number stays visible above it), except for 5 s after each XP change. With no waypoints the fill bar is back.
- A wall map at the base must be its own map id (a framed map adds a frame marker to every copy of that id).
- Locking a map in a cartography table creates a NEW map id (the original stays unlocked). Use the locked copy's id in `city-maps` (plugins\DonatingPhone\config.yml).
- Paper (javap): a map with a custom renderer renders for a carrier when its pixels are dirty or every 5th tick, and sends icons every 5th tick even while the map is pocketed (the plugin keeps the last icons then; the client only draws a held map). `Player#sendMap` sends the whole map at once (used when the phone comes out). `Player#lookAt` sends the look-at packet, which turns the client camera.
- Held maps render two-handed (big, centered) only with an empty offhand. Players with a bag see the phone one-handed (small, lower corner); players without a bag always hold it two-handed.
- Locator bar: vanilla hides a player's dot while they sneak, are invisible, or wear a head/skull or carved pumpkin (a HEAD-slot `waypoint_transmit_range_hide` modifier).
- Shader minimaps (NMinimap and similar): 26.3 compiles pack shaders to SPIR-V; old `#moj_import` shaders fail and the client rejects the whole server pack.
