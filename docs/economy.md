### Shops (shop.sk, built 2026-09-25)
- Data: `wpn::<W>` (unlocked weapon), `loadout::<n>` + `::count` + `loadout::saved` (Save button), `bag-tier` / `bag-best`. Equipped weapons, loaded rounds, ammo and gear live only in the inventory (death.sk clears them).
- Every purchase: check, charge (`chargeMoney` in core.sk: all or nothing, compares the balance before and after), grant, verify, refund if the grant failed, all in one call with no wait; logged to `plugins\Skript\logs\shop.log`.
- One action per drawn screen: each click is handled a tick later and only if the screen generation is unchanged, so a same-tick double click buys once (tested). Clicks on the player's own inventory, number keys and double clicks do nothing; every click is cancelled, also for staff with the bypass.
- The hotbar mirror (2026-09-29): each slot is named by what it holds with one hint: a weapon "Click: take out", a consumable or tool "Click: throw away" (a second click), any slot while a weapon is selected "Click: put the <gun> here" (onto a consumable or tool: the two swap when the weapon was in the hotbar, else the consumable moves to a free hotbar 1-5 slot). The gear shop shows each item's real cut (armor points × Per_Armor_Point 6%: Guns -12% / -18% / -30% / -48%).
- Buys of $1,000 or more (PROPOSAL `shop::confirm-above`), throwing Stims away, replacing worn gear and swapping bags need a second click within 5 s. While armed, that button shows a green "Confirm purchase?" block (a red "Throw away?" block on the hotbar mirror) and turns back after the window (owner, 2026-09-25). Too little money: refused on the first click with the decline sound, no confirm (owner, 2026-09-25). The confirm key is the button's action + the price, so it must match the action shopAction compares against.
- One stack per consumable: Save keeps each consumable once, and Restore only refills the saved slot when that consumable isn't carried in another slot.
- Selling back (owner, 2026-10-22: "sell their bag or gun for 50% of the buying price", the unlock stays, buying again at full price; shop.sk sellWeapon, sellBag, core.sk `shop::sell-share` 0.5): a right-click on an owned gun (Weapons tab) or the carried bag (bag shop), a second click within 5 s (an orange "Sell it?" block). A gun pays half of what was last paid (data `wpn-paid::<W>`, set at every unlock; 0 for Rusty's knife and staff unlocks; one unlocked before has none: its price), leaves hotbar 1-5 and becomes data `wpn::<W>` "sold" (not owned: buyWeapon skips the level check for it, the Weapons tab says "Sold: no level needed"). A bag pays half its tier's replacement price (selling a just-unlocked bag and rebuying gains nothing), `bag-best` stays. Refused with loot carried, in combat (shopBlockReason) and inside the prison (shopJailBlock). bots
un.js shop 61.
- Purchases check `chargeMoney(...) is not true` (fail closed): a missing price setting means the call never runs, and `is false` would then let the buy through for free.
- `auditWeapons()` on join and when a shop opens: removes WeaponMechanics items outside hotbar 1-5, second copies, weapons that aren't unlocked, and trims consumable stacks over the max (not for staff with the bypass).
- Shopkeepers are Paper 1.21.9+ mannequins (look like players, no AI, no locator-bar dot). No shopping while combat-tagged or wanted, and getting hurt into combat closes an open shop.
- Prices (owner, 2026-09-26, reworked 2026-09-27): weapons cost more and guns need a level, so new robbers start with melee (Combat Knife $500 L0, Classic Pistol (.50 GS) $7,500 L5, Old Revolver $20,000 L10, Machine Gun (Uzi) $45,000 L15, Brave Patriot $80,000 L25, Shotgun (R9-0) $110,000 L30, Combat Rifle $160,000 L40, AK-48 $225,000 L50, Sniper Rifle $500,000 L70, Veteran $800,000 L90, Hand Minigun $1,500,000 L125 (the five added 2026-10-06 and the minigun, 2026-10-12, are PROPOSALs): core.sk `wpn::<W>::price|level`; the first unlock checks the level, owning one never does); helmets $1,500 / $5,000, vests $2,000 / $10,000, a Stim $400 (doubled); bags unlock at 120% of what they hold ($2,400 / $7,200 / $18,000 / $48,000 / $120,000) and need a level for the first unlock of a tier (`bag::<t>::level` 0 / 5 / 10 / 25 / 60; a new one after losing it needs none and costs 20%: $400 / $1,200 / $3,000 / $8,000 / $20,000). No bag is ever free (owner, 2026-09-27; `bag::free-if-broke` is gone): with none the hands carry up to $1,000 (The bag, below). Start money $1,000 (the tutorial's first car job pays the rest of the Gym Bag).
- Adding a gun later: pick it from WeaponMechanics' list, add `Reload.Ammo` (an existing or new ammo type in Donating_Ammos.yml), `Swap_Hands: true` and a Donating bullet (`Projectile:` from Donating_Projectiles.yml, see Bullets) to its file, add its `wpn::<W>::*` settings (with `model` = its `Skin: Default` number, for the shop icon) and its title to `shop::weapons` in core.sk, then add it to bots\scenarios\wm-ammo.js.
- Heist tools (2026-09-25, for loot.sk): the Drill ($2,500, carry 1) and the Safe Kit ($400, carry 3), PROPOSALs in core.sk `tool::<t>::*`. Sold in the tools shop and the gun shop's Items tab (click 1, shift-click fill up), one stack per tool in the first free hotbar 1-5 slot, thrown away from the hotbar mirror (second click), trimmed or removed by `auditTools` (in `auditWeapons`), lost on death (death.sk clears 0-4), not in Save/Restore. `toolCount`, `toolUse` (lowers the stack) and `toolItem` are for loot.sk.

### Robber levels (levels.sk, built 2026-09-26)
- Built as "robber ranks" and renamed to levels the same day (owner: paid ranks show in the tab list, levels stay in the footer; two different things, two different words). Each level has a title (Pickpocket ... Kingpin).
- XP comes from selling loot at the base (bag.sk's bagSell calls levelSale last, so a level-up's title replaces the sale's): X = ⌊P / 100⌋ + 10 × H, P = what the sale paid for loot the seller robbed themselves (progress.sk's own-loot ledger, since the abuse review 2026-09-28: a picked-up duffel pays money, never levels; passive players get 75%, so their XP too), H = the heist runs in the sale with own loot not credited to this player before (data `level-haul::<h>` = the last run credited; selling one run's loot in several trips pays the +10 once). E.g. a full Duffel Bag ($6,000) from one heist: 60 + 10 = 70 XP. No XP for kills (it would pay for griefing). Tests add own loot with `/zzbagadd <p> <tag> <$> own`.
- Levels (owner, 2026-09-27: "a lot of levels, like at the end of the month, someone who plays like 2 hours a day will be around level 150"; 2026-09-28: "each level should get a little harder each time to level up, like a 12% increase each level"): level 1 needs `level::first` (49) XP, and each level after needs g more than the one before, g easing linearly from `level::growth-start` 8% (level 2) to `level::growth-end` 2% at `level::growth-until` 60 and flat after (PROPOSAL; a flat 12% is impossible: 1.12^149 ≈ 22 million times level 1's, and a 12% start made levels 1-30 take under 2 h). T(n) = the running sum, rounded once (levels.sk levelTable, cached in `{-level::t::<n>}`, levelFor a binary search): 1 = 49, 5 = 287, 10 = 700, 15 = 1,281, 20 = 2,078, 30 = 4,543, 45 = 11,234, 50 = 14,422, 60 = 22,253, 100 = 75,514, 125 = 137,877, 150 = 240,190, 200 = 683,428, 250 = 1,876,444, 300 = 5,087,552 XP. tools\progress-sim.js (median 2 h/day): level 5 at 0.5 h, 20 at 1.8 h, 45 at 5.5 h, 60 at 7.1 h, 100 at 18.9 h, 150 at 57.8 h. `level::max` 500 is only a technical bound. Titles by range (`level::brackets` "from|name|color"): 0 Pickpocket, 5 Shoplifter, 20 Burglar, 45 Safecracker, 60 Heister, 100 Mastermind, 150 Kingpin, 200 Godfather. /levels lists only the levels that unlock something (levelMilestones). Retune after the beta with the growth settings (lowering is safe, raising strands players because a level never drops).
- Rewards past 100 (owner, 2026-09-28: "think of some good rewards past 100 and 150, something new added to the map, or something that unlocks"; `level::rewards` "level|kind|what[|n]", given once each: data `lvlreward::<line>`, also caught up at a join): bound titles (rarity "level") «Mastermind» 100, «High Roller» 125, «Kingpin» 150, «Street King» 175, «Godfather» 200, «Made of Teflon» 250, «Urban Myth» 300; kill effects Crown 150 and Money Rain 200; crate keys (100: 2 Epic; 125: a Legendary; 150: 2; 175: 2; 200: 3; 250: 3; 300: 5). Map unlocks: members-only areas, WorldGuard regions `lvl<N>_<name>` (levels.sk: walking, teleports and cars pushed back; a passenger's car stops at the border; a backstop every second moves anyone standing where they may not (a rejoin inside, a region drawn around them) to their last allowed spot or spawn; staff always pass). Proposed builds (DEPLOY.md): a level-150 lounge, and "The Reserve", a heist for level 100+ (`/dheist set <id> level 100`, a bigger pool). XP: sales unchanged (floor(P / 100) + 10 per new run), car contracts pay / 50 (30 / 90 / 250), hits 100 / 250 / 600, Mara's chapter ends 150 / 600 / 1,500 / 3,000 / 4,000, Vic's missions 250 / 400 / 500 / 750, contact jobs haul 100, safe 150, vault 200, getaway 200, Boss 30 / 45 / 75. Designed by a workflow (2 designers + a critic/synthesis, checked in the code); the invariants: Mara's c1_level = d2's level, c2_level = d3's, c3_level = d4's = fence::level = Vic's start; basic <= c1, pro <= c3, master <= c5; Safe Kit <= c1, Drill <= c2.
- What a level unlocks (2026-09-28 scale): heists (heistLevel(h): `/dheist set <id> level <n>`, else the difficulty's default: 0 / 0 / 5 / 20 / 45 for stores and difficulty 1-4, owner 2026-09-28; a store had a max level, 9, until 2026-10-21 (heistLevelOk is levelOk now, so the story, jobs, /heists, tab completion and Heist Refresh agree); the entry refusal names the level), heist tools (Safe Kit 5, Drill 15, Grappler 30), weapons (Combat Knife 0, Pipe Wrench 0, Brass Knuckles 2, Katana 12, Gold Nunchucks 15, Classic Pistol 5, Old Revolver 10, Machine Gun 15, Brave Patriot 25, Shotgun 30, Combat Rifle 40, AK-48 50, Sniper Rifle 70, Veteran 90, Hand Minigun 125), consumables (Bandage 0, Throwing Knife 5, Energy Drink 10: shop.sk conLevel, also Restore loadout and crates), bag tiers (0 / 5 / 10 / 25 / 60), car jobs (Street 5, Pro 30, Master 60; the tutorial's first job 0), hit contracts (15 / 30 / 50), Vic (45), cars to drive (Sedan 20, Sport Sedan 35, Jeep 50, Sports Car 75, Hotrod 100, Viper 110, Riviera 130, SUV 150, Apex 150; a crate car above your level waits in the garage), the level rewards above. The invariants: Mara's c1_level = d2's level (5), c2_level = d3's (20), c3_level = d4's = fence::level = Vic's start (45). A map's heists set with an old /dheist level must be set again on the new scale.
- XP is never taken away and a level never drops by itself; only staff move a level down (`/dlevel set` puts the XP at that level's start, so the two stay in step; `/dlevel reset` also clears the credited runs). Every 5 s (and on join) a player whose XP is worth a higher level than they're saved at is caught up (the thresholds were lowered), and the footer copies follow a settings reload. A level whose XP setting is missing can't be reached (fails closed). A level-up goes to that player only (owner, 2026-09-26: level-ups stay quiet): the LEVEL UP title, a sound, and each unlock in chat.
- Saved: data `level-xp`, `level`, `level-haul::<h>` (data saved under the old names `rank-xp` / `rank` moves over once on join; a heist's old `{heist::<id>::rank}` moves to `level` when heists.sk loads). TAB asks from its own thread, so no placeholder may call a function (a function being reloaded is missing for a moment: a Severe Error on TAB's thread, seen 2026-09-26); placeholders.sk copies the balance, the bounty's money texts and the heist name into memory once a second. Log: plugins\Skript\logs\levels.log.
- Players: `/level` (alias `/lvl`: level, XP, a progress bar, what the next level unlocks), `/levels` (every level). The phone's stats and the tab-list footer show it ("Level 2 Burglar · 1,240/3,000 XP"); /help mentions it. Staff: `/dlevel info|set|xp|reset <player> [value]` (`xp` only adds).
- The test heists in bots\scenarios set `level 0` for themselves (the difficulty defaults would lock the bots out); heists.js checks the default (difficulty 3: level 2).
- Local: the owner's account (Explosde) starts at level 0 like everyone. `/dlevel set Explosde 150` skips the grind for playtests.

### Paid ranks (ranks.sk, built 2026-09-26)
- Owner, 2026-09-26: "make separate monetized ranks that show next to tab list (higher ranks are higher on the tab list)". A tag before the name in the tab list, in chat and in join messages, a spot higher in the tab list, a bag skin and more bag room (Rank bags below; Monetization rules). No money, guns, bags or levels. Chat: LPC's `group-formats` give owner and the paid ranks "{prefix}&r &f{name}" ("[Legend] Name", the name white; the prefixes have no trailing space). Copy LPC's config.yml to Minehut.
- Each rank is a LuckPerms group (PROPOSAL, core.sk `paidranks` listed highest first, `paidrank::<id>::name|prefix|weight`): legend "&d[Legend]" 40, elite "&6[Elite]" 30, vipplus "&b[VIP+]" 20, vip "&a[VIP]" 10. The weight orders the groups and is also the prefix's priority, so the highest rank's tag shows and staff (owner, weight 100) beat every paid one. The owner sets the names and prices.
- TAB's sorting (plugins\TAB\config.yml, sorting-types): `GROUPS:owner,admin,mod,helper,builder,legend,elite,vipplus,vip,default`, then the biggest bounty, then A-Z. TAB reads the LuckPerms primary group (the highest weight), so a new paid rank's id must be added there too. The tab name's tag is TAB's `%condition:rankprefix%` (the LuckPerms prefix + a space).
- `/dranks setup` (staff/console) creates or updates the groups from core.sk (`lp creategroup`, `setweight`, `setdisplayname`, `meta setprefix <weight> "<prefix>"`). LuckPerms data is per server: run it once on Minehut. `/dranks give <player> <id|none>` removes every other paid group, then adds that one (one paid rank at a time), by UUID (LuckPerms only knows the names of players who joined; a store may sell to someone who hasn't), but a player who already has a higher rank keeps it (data `paid-rank` records the rank given: LuckPerms can't be asked reliably about offline players); `/dranks take <player> <id>` removes that rank only if it's the player's (Tebex's refund/chargeback command). /dranks and /dbooster need `donating.store` (the owner through `*`, and the console), never plain staff: they grant paid perks; a store (Tebex or similar, not chosen yet) would run it from the console on a purchase. `/dranks list` shows who online has which. Logged to plugins\Skript\logs\ranks.log. LuckPerms runs its commands asynchronously: a change shows a moment later.
- Players: `/ranks` (the ranks highest first with their tag, bag skin and bag bonus; yours; the store; a pointer to /level), `/store` (alias `/donate`: the Tebex store's address from core.sk `store::url`, "opening soon" while empty). `paidRank(p)` reads the player's LuckPerms groups through Vault (`groups of`) and data `paid-rank` (the purchase record), the higher weight winning (owner, 2026-10-21: "people who get another higher rank like admin still have their permissions from the rank they bought": staff moved to another group keep /heistrefresh, Valet, daily keys, the bag bonus and skins; `/dranks take` and `give none` clear the record).
- Rank bags (owner, 2026-09-26: "custom (but balanced) bags"): a rank gives every bag the player carries more room, capacity × (1 + b), b = `paidrank::<id>::bag-bonus` (VIP 0.05, VIP+ 0.1, Elite 0.2, Legend 0.25: owner, 2026-09-26, raised from 0.15 / 0.2), and its look (`paidrank::<id>::skin`: camo, arctic, gilded, neon; the art is make-item-art.js's BAGS; Neon's model glows with light_emission). A rank has its own and every lower rank's skin, plus any bag skins from crates (cosmetics.sk); `/bagskin [name|default]` picks one by id or name (data `bag-skin`); crate skins work without a rank.
- More rank perks (owner, 2026-09-26): crate keys with every /daily (`paidrank::<id>::daily-keys`: VIP+ Common; Elite Common, Uncommon; Legend Common, Uncommon, Rare; crates.sk), Heist Refresh (`paidrank::<id>::refresh-hours`: Elite 12, Legend 6; below), and Legends own every retired and testing cosmetic (`paidrank::legend::retired-cosmetics`, cosmetics.sk). /ranks lists them per rank.
- Serials (owner, 2026-09-27: rare items tracked so their value can be judged in trades, "#1/1" growing as more people unlock them): every tradeable copy (a crate cosmetic; a crate car, garage.sk) gets the next number when it's first unlocked: `{serial::cos:<id>}` / `{serial::car:<id>}` count the copies, data `cosserial::<id>` / `carserial::<id>` (per model, so a repaint or a remade plate keeps it) the copy's number; a trade moves it; a rank's or an earned (bound) item has none; a copy from before serials is numbered when first shown. "#3/57" after the name in the wardrobe lore ("Serial #3 of 57 so far", "(retired: no more are made)"), the trade window, the garage, the crate pull line and the Legendary/Hacked announcement. crates.js and trade.js check it.
- Heist Refresh (ranks.sk `/heistrefresh [heist]`): reopens a heist in cooldown for everyone through heists.sk's `heistRefresh(h)` (like `/dheist open`: it opens once the room reset is done, with the usual "is open" announcement, plus "<name> refreshed the <heist>"). Only a heist in cooldown (never open or running), never while its alarm is still running (opening calls heistEndAlarm, which would end the chase of robbers who got out: a paid way to lose the cops), never one whose reopen time already passed (the room is resetting after a restart, or someone just refreshed it: `heistRefreshBlock` says "opening" and the refresh isn't used up), not while the player is combat-tagged, only one the player's level allows, no head start; data `refresh-at` (unix seconds) and the current rank's hours decide the next one; logged to ranks.log. Id or name, spaces optional. All capacity goes through bag.sk's `bagCapacity(p)` (the bag's %, room, the phone, the bag shop's lore, and death.sk's loss cap C, so a bigger bag risks more). Bags are still bought with in-game money and lost on death. A rank bought or lost shows on the bag within 5 s (every 5 seconds, bagRefresh, which only redraws on a change; its key is tier:total:skin:capacity). LuckPerms' `vault-group-use-displaynames: true` makes Vault return display names ("VIP+"), so a group matches by id or by name.
- Verified with bots (bots\run.js ranks, 11 checks): the tags in the tab list (player_info display names) and in chat; the client's order (26.3 PlayerTabOverlay sorts by list order, then team name, then the name; TAB's teams carry the order) puts Legend over VIP over a no-rank player who has the biggest bounty and comes first A-Z, and staff above all; a new rank replaces the old one; "none" removes it and the bounty decides again.

### Boosters (boosters.sk, built 2026-09-26; XP and Heist Rush 2026-09-27)
- Owner, 2026-09-27: "want to add other boosters so i can make a booster category in the tebex". Three kinds (core.sk `booster::kinds`, `booster::<k>::name|max-multiplier|buyer-bonus`): **money** (below), **xp** (robber-level XP from sales and contracts × b for everyone, the buyer's × (b + 0.5); `levelAddXp` applies it through `levelBoosted`, never to staff grants; the sale's chat line and the contract title show the boosted XP) and **rush** (Heist Rush: `heistEndRun` uses `heistCooldownNow` = cooldown / b, and `heistRushStart` cuts every running cooldown's time left by b when one starts; no buyer bonus: balanced for everyone, like Heist Refresh). One of each kind runs at a time, each with its own queue (`{booster::<prefix>active|left|queue::*}`, prefix "" for money so the first boosters' saved names stay, "<k>::" for the rest); the footer lists every running one. `dbooster add <player> [money|xp|rush] <multiplier> <minutes> [count]` (no kind: money), `stop|clear|info [kind]` (no kind: money). Tebex descriptions for all three and for each crate key are in MONETIZATION.md. Verified with bots (bots\run.js boosters, 22 checks).
- Owner, 2026-09-26: "someone buys 1.5x money for the server for 30 mins and since they bought it for everyone they get 2x instead of 1.5x"; "$1/5min boost". While a booster runs, every loot sale pays P' = floor(P × b) for everyone and floor(P × (b + `booster::buyer-bonus`)) for its buyer (0.5; 0 would make it the same for all). P is the normal pay (passive players' 75% included); level XP and the robbery bounty stay on P. bagSell logs `pay=`, `extra=`, `booster=`.
- Delivered by Tebex: `dbooster add {username} 1.5 5 {purchaseQuantity}` (minutes × count, count 1-10,000; split into queued boosters of at most `booster::max-minutes` 240; the multiplier is rounded to 2 decimals, then must be over 1 and at most `booster::max-multiplier` 2). Every request and refusal is logged (a refused paid delivery must leave a trace), and so are staff stops and each cleared queue entry. One runs at a time, the rest queue (keys 100001, 100002, ... so they sort as text). The time counts down only while someone is online (Minehut sleeps an empty server).
- Saved, so a paid booster survives restarts and sleep: `{booster::active}` "name|uuid|b", `{booster::left}` seconds, `{booster::queue::<n>}` "name|uuid|b|seconds". The start and end are announced to everyone (the only non-heist broadcasts besides bounty claims and combat logs); the tab-list footer's last line is `%donating_booster%` ("⚡ 1.5× money for everyone · 23:10 · thanks Name", empty otherwise; copied to memory once a second). Players: `/booster`. Staff: `/dbooster add|stop|clear|info`. Log: plugins\Skript\logs\boosters.log.
- Verified with bots (bots\run.js boosters, 16 checks; the state is checked in variables.csv, not only after a reload): 1.5× for everyone and 2× for the buyer on a $1,000 sale, XP unchanged, the announcements and the footer, the queue, running out, refusals, staff only, surviving a reload.

### Crates, keys and /daily (crates.sk, built 2026-09-26)
- Owner, 2026-09-26: crates daily, common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary and hacked (special events only, never sold); /daily gives everyone a daily key (a crate of its own, not a Common one) plus the rank's keys; keys for common to legendary are sold on Tebex (the owner chose that knowing Tebex's rules forbid paid random rewards: see Tebex below). Rewards: core.sk `crate::<id>::rewards` = "weight|kind|what|amount" (money|$, ammo|type|rounds, con|W|n, tool|t|n, cos|id[,id...] = a set (its icon is its bag skin, else its kill effect, else the title: two title-first sets made the Hacked spin look frozen), car|id|Name); chance = weight / the crate's total, shown in the preview; every crate's weights add up to 1000 (30 = 3%). Rarity names and colors: `rarity::<r>::name|color|dupe`.
- The tables (owner, 2026-09-26, PROPOSAL amounts): money 55-60% lower than before but 60-75% of openings from Common to Legendary; cosmetics at most 1 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 10 / 15% (Daily ... Legendary), every cosmetic in one crate only (Daily has its own: Rookie, Poof, Denim, rarity "daily"); Legendary has the exclusive car Vandal at 3% (inside its 15%); Hacked is 5 prizes at 20% each: the cars Vandal, Specter, Overclock (placeholders) and two sets, H4CK3R + Matrix and Zero Day + Glitch (a set gives the parts you don't have; only a set you fully own pays the dupe). - Opening only at crate stands (owner, 2026-09-26: "special places on the map"; `/crates` and the phone's Crates app only show keys, odds, the Store and Buy a key buttons). `crateOpen`: refused while dead, spinning or with a reward pending, away from the stand (`crate::stand-reach` 6 blocks), inside a heist, combat-tagged, or outside a safe zone (owner: "Only in safe zones"; `/dcrate place` warns about a stand outside one); then the key and the roll in one call with no wait, saved as the pending reward `{cratepending::<uuid>::crate|line}` and logged (`open <name> <uuid> <crate> keys-left= line=`). ### Seasons and leaderboards (seasons.sk, progress.sk, built 2026-09-27)
- Owner, 2026-09-27: "need to implement more competition ... seasons, leaderboard, and rewards for being the top"; then "3 different leaderboards with different metrics being measured, earned titles untradable, close farming loops (except for heist refresh since its balanced for everyone), dont leave out things from the paid ranks". Designed from a draft critiqued by 3 critics and a synthesis, then fitted to those answers.
- **progress.sk** is the hub: the scripts call `progEvent(p, ev, n, detail)` last at each moment (every argument set: a function with an unset one isn't called): `rob` (loot.sk lootTake, "<h>#<run>"), `sell` (bag.sk bagSell, once per heist run in the sale, the run's loot before passive and boosters), `crack` (lootCrack), `drill` (lootDrillDone, only for the owner who gets the head start), `getaway` (heists.sk heistRelease with reason "range"), `contract` (ctDeliver, the pay and the tier). Lifetime counters: data life::crack|drill|getaway|contract. The story will listen here too.
- **Soft seasons** (`season::days` 28, PROPOSAL): only the boards reset. Three boards, three metrics (core.sk `season::board::<b>::*`): **Top Earners** (earners: money from loot sold at its normal price, a rank's bigger bag included, passive players' 75%, never a money booster's extra; plus contract pay), **Master Thieves** (heisters: a heist run pays up to w_d = 25 / 50 / 90 / 150 points for difficulty 1-4, reached once full = $1,000 / $3,000 / $7,500 / $20,000 of that run's loot is sold, w_d × min(1, V / full), credited as it grows so several trips pay what one would; safe 10, vault 25, getaway 40) and **Wheelmen** (wheelmen: contracts 20 / 40 / 80 by tier).
- **Farming loops closed:** loot counts only up to the dollars the player took from that run themselves (`rob` carries the $; `{ss::<n>::robbedv|soldv::<u>::<h>#<run>}`; a duffel picked up from someone else, even of the same run, pays money but no score; loot robbed just before a season ended counts when sold in the next); the per-run cap (one piece per heist or tiny sales add nothing); a getaway once per run (`{ss::<n>::esc::<u>::<h>}` = run), with that run's loot in the bag, loot the player robbed in that run, after a wave came (heistWave ≥ 1); a run sold partly while passive keeps the lower share; a heist deleted and made again keeps its run counter (no shared "<id>#<run>"); passive players × 0.75 on every board; staff with `donating.season.exempt` (the owner through `*`) aren't counted; a Heist Refresh run counts like any other (owner). A safe opens once per run for everyone, a vault once per run.
- **Ranking:** a changed board is re-ranked every 5 s (one board per pass) with `sorted indices`; ties go to whoever got there first (value + 0.5 × the share of the season left at the last increase). Memory: `{-lb::rank::<b>::<u>}`, `{-lb::top::<b>::<r>}`, `{-ph::lb::<b>::<r>}` (placeholders `%donating_lb_<board>_<rank>%`, `%donating_season%` = "S1 · 12d 4h · #8 Master Thieves" on the main sidebar, `%donating_season_short%`). ### Trading (trade.sk, built 2026-09-26)
- Owner, 2026-09-26: players trade **cars** and **crate cosmetics**, **in person**, with **no tax**, and never in-game money ("not their money interacting with the server"). The owner's plan: trading like CS:GO skins, where players pay each other real money outside the game for rare items; the server only ever moves the items. Told (2026-09-26): Mojang's usage guidelines say in-game items and currencies must have no real-world value and can't be cashed out, and count gambling as harming their brand; items from paid random crates that sell for real money is the CS:GO skin-gambling pattern (lawsuits and regulators); no escrow means scams between players (the log helps staff settle disputes); many players are young. The owner decides; nothing in the game mentions real money.
- `/trade <player>` within `trade::reach` (5) blocks asks them; `/trade <you>` back within `trade::request-time` (30 s) opens the window for both (6 rows, "Trade · <name>": your offer left, theirs right, a black divider). "Add a car" lists your cars (in the garage, not out or driven; the other player must meet a dealer car's level, crate cars need none; if they have that model already the list says they must give theirs in the same trade: two players can swap Sedans), "Add a cosmetic" your crate cosmetics (saved per player; never a rank's bag skins or a Legend's retired ones; not one they own). Each entry says why not when it can't be added. Click one of yours to take it back. Up to 16 a side; a one-sided trade is a gift.
- Ready (slot 48; the other's state at 50): any change (adding, taking back, un-ready) makes both not ready, and Ready is refused for 1 s after a change (a click aimed at the old offer, or delayed by ping, can't agree to a new one) and while the swap couldn't happen (`tradeCheck`: "TradeB has a Sedan already" until they add theirs). Both ready: a countdown (`trade::countdown` 5 s, a clock at 49) and then the swap in one call with no wait, re-checking everything (next to each other, out of combat and heists; every entry still its giver's and still receivable); MTVehicles cars it lost are made again first (carEnsure). All entries leave their givers before any arrives (a same-model swap works). A car keeps its plate: garage data, `{car::owner}`, locked, no members, and MTVehicles' owner (`set vehicle owner`). A cosmetic's data moves; a worn title, kill effect or bag skin comes off the giver.
- Ends with no swap when either closes the window (switching to an add list doesn't; the close marks the view "closed" first, so a redraw in the 1-tick gap can't reopen it), types `/trade cancel`, walks away (reach + 3), is hurt, combat-tagged, dies, logs out or is in a heist (checked every second). Memory only; logged to plugins\Skript\logs\trade.log (open, trade with every entry and plate, refused, cancel), plus trade-out/trade-in lines in garage.log and cosmetics.log. MTVehicles saves owners only every 10 minutes, so garage.sk's enter handler sets the owner again whenever the owner gets in (a crash after a trade can't leave the car undrivable). A car MTVehicles lost and couldn't remake isn't traded (`carAlive`).
- Verified with bots (bots\run.js trade, 18 checks: PLAYTEST 119) and in the real 26.3 client (PLAYTEST 120). Reviewed (3 reviewers + a skeptic, 2026-09-27): 7 distinct problems confirmed, all fixed (the MTVehicles owner after a crash, Esc reopened by a redraw during the countdown, Ready landing on a changed offer, same-model swaps impossible, a lost car tradeable, garage.log missing the giver, "cosmetics are never taken away").
