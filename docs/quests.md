### The city clock (clock.sk, 2026-10-21; rebuilt on vanilla time the same day: PLAYTEST 249-250)
- The owner's clock (Game design: The city clock). First built (a cloud session) as a game minute a real second, driving every world's sky with Paper 26.1's `time rate 0.833333` and a `time set` whenever it drifted 3 game minutes; the owner, after playing: "the clock time is making the server lag, build it around the default minecraft day/night cycle time". Now it only reads.
- The time of day is the play world's (clock::world, else prison::world "jailbreak", else world): "%time in world%" (H:mm, 0 ticks = 6:00 AM) as game minutes 0-1439, vanilla pace (a day 20 real minutes, `clock::rate` 1.2 game minutes a real second, used only to turn game time into real countdowns). Days: saved `{clock::day}`, +1 whenever the time of day goes back more than 2 minutes between two passes (midnight, a /time set or /dclock set to an earlier time, a night slept through: each is a new day). G = day × 1440 + the time of day; the day's name floor(G / 1440) through clock::days ("Tue"), the week floor(G / 10080) (140 real minutes). clockSecs() = G × 60 (hits.sk's windows), clockText(m) "3:45 PM", clockNight(m) (7 PM to 5 AM), clockWeekLeft() (real seconds to the next game week), clockRealSecs(game seconds) = ceil(g / (60 × rate)). It never sets a world's time or speed except on `/dclock set`; beds work (the time jumps to morning: a new day). The worlds were put back to `time rate 1`.
- Shown: the sidebar's first line `%donating_clock%` ("☀ Tue 3:45 PM", "☾" at night; clock.sk's own placeholder handler, memory only), `%donating_clock_time%`, the phone's status bar (ui.sk phoneClock), `/clock` (the time, the day, "A day is 20 real minutes.", dawn or night in real minutes). Staff `/dclock info|set <h:mm> [am|pm]|day <n>` (set: the play world's time, an earlier one starts a new day; day: the day count). Log: clock.log.
- On the game clock: progress.sk progWeek() = clockWeek() (the hit contracts' target and windows, Vic's Wanted List, the heists' weekly heat; `{-prog::weekoverride}` for tests); hits.sk's windows in game seconds (a week 604800, a day 86400 of them), their starts so a window fits its half day or day (lg = hit::window-length × 60 × rate game seconds), hit::window-length 300 real seconds of active time (6 game hours; was 3600), a streak in a row while the gap is at most hit::streak-gap (8) game weeks; fence.sk fenceWeekLeft = clockWeekLeft, fenceLeftText "12m" under an hour. Vic's "New names every Thursday" line says "every week". On real time: /daily, contract cooldowns, contact jobs, seasons, boosters, messages' "ago".
- tools\progress-sim.js: a hit contract once a game week (8,400 real seconds) while playing (hitTime 900 s; was once a real week, 1200 s); a store trip's overhead 110 s and death 4% (the police). 60 h median: hits $2.85M of $21.88M (were $240K of $22.85M), level 149 (was 151).
- Tests: bots\run.js clock (7 checks: the world's time, /dclock set, vanilla speed, the night icon, midnight adding a day, /clock), progress's week check (/dclock day moves the week); both pass 2026-10-21. Test helpers `/zzclock [world]`, `/zzweek` (day=).

### Quest research and the owner's picks (2026-09-27)
- Owner, 2026-09-27: "for this story mode/ side story quests, i want to make it actually worthwhile, so research different types of story mode quests from gta or other stuff and find which styles have the most player retention rate and brainstorm a couple ideas for me to pick out". Researched (3 researchers, a designer and a critic; sources in the session's scratchpad, sq-research.txt): no studio publishes retention per quest style. The best evidence: streaks that forgive a missed day (Duolingo A/B tests, learning apps), a story arc that ends by unlocking a repeatable loop (GTA Online's Casino, The Contract, Cayo Perico; publisher statements and SteamCharts, confounded by holidays), daily boards where jobs wait instead of expiring (WoW's emissaries), and social play for players past the early game (correlational). Weak or negative: a weekly time-gated story drip, one-time live events (spikes only), mastery challenges, collectibles. The rule that came out of it: the story is the wrapper, the repeatable loop is the product; everything solo; nothing expires hard; quests reward heists and car jobs rather than replace them.
- The owner picked **Contact Jobs** (daily jobs with twists from Mara and the Scrap Yard Boss, 3 banked per contact, rep for titles) and **Vic the Fence** (a contact arc from level 3 that unlocks a weekly Wanted List), social quests (a crew getaway bonus, a server-wide City Job) later once the server has regulars, and "something else". Not picked: the Street Cred streak, Mara's Scores (prep + finale).
- The owner's "something else" (2026-09-27): "maybe take on a hitman for a specific npc quest for the week, different difficulties (easy = npc spawns twice a day randomly for an hour and roams the city, medium= once a day, hard = 5 times spread out through the real life week, and the reward will get better with harder difficulty), also keep in mind all the player specific renders/interactions needed for this, also think about player specific things like the city garage showing player specific inventory(im going to make more things in game instead of easy access in the menu, maybe have the easy access menu for high paid ranks)". So: weekly hitman contracts; a per-player rendering layer (things only one player sees or can touch); an in-world garage showing each player's own cars; menus moving into the world, with remote ("easy access") menus as a perk for high paid ranks (a gameplay convenience: MONETIZATION.md must list it). Designed (two design workflows, 2026-09-27; specs in the session scratchpad, spec-jobs.txt and spec-hitman.txt).
- The owner's answers on the hitman design (2026-09-27):
  - Sightings: "shared clock windows but only while server is active (2+ players or any specific player with the same difficulty hitman quest active is online) and not asleep". Built as: every window (Easy 2 a day, Medium 1 a day, Hard 5 a week) has a shared random start time; it runs only while the server is active for it (2+ players online, or a holder of a contract of that difficulty online); a window whose time comes while the server isn't active waits until it is, and its hour counts only active time.
  - Fighting back: "bodyguards have guns and are the same strength (medium), just the thing that makes it harder it catching the npc on time". Every target has armed bodyguards of the same (medium) strength on every tier; the difficulty is how rarely the target shows up. The bodyguards' bullets must be hidden from everyone but the hunter and must never hurt bystanders (DonatingPhone, Java).
  - Valet: yes, Elite and Legend call their car anywhere (never in heists, combat or while wanted); everyone else takes cars out at a garage in person. A new paid convenience: MONETIZATION.md gets a Valet line and the owner sends it to Minehut again.
  - A parked car waits while its owner is within 64 blocks or inside a heist (the getaway car stays outside).
  - Not asked, the recommended defaults: one contract a week (switching tier allowed until the takedown), rewards (built as $10,000 / $25,000 / $60,000 + a Season key on Hard, PROPOSAL), the words "Hit contracts" and "TARGET DOWN" (never "kill"), the phone plugin extended for per-player hiding, bounties posted in person with the Broker (the list stays on the phone). Contact Jobs and Vic use the spec's defaults (Mara's board when her story is done, the Boss's after the first delivered contract, and so on).

### Contact jobs (jobs.sk, built 2026-09-27)
- Each contact (Mara, the Scrap Yard Boss) leaves each player a job every `jobs::hours` (6: owner, 2026-09-27); up to 3 wait (4 at Family rep) and never expire; a full board wastes arrivals. Boards open: the Boss's after the first delivered car job, Mara's when her story is done. Goal N = max(500, floor100(min(run::d, 0.6 × best bag, 0.25 × pool))); pay R = max(500, floor50(β × S)) (S = N, or the contract's pay); paid P = floor(R × (1 + 0.5K) × (1 + 0.25C) × m) (K = the ★ bonus kept: In and Out, Quiet Hands, Ghost, Code Breaker, Wheelman, Speed Run, Clean Pick; C = a cold heist nobody robbed in 24 h; m = 0.75 passive). Never boosted, no season points, no bounty. Rep (10-20 a job, +5 for a bonus): Trusted 400, Partner 1,250, Family 3,000 (x2.5 with jobs every 6 h) give bound titles (rep_mara_2..4, rep_boss_2..4) and at Family a 4th slot. - An event counts for one job per contact: the tracked job first, then Special Orders, then slot order. A met job pays in the pass (≥ 3 whole seconds later: "paying" first, so a crash never pays twice). Swap once a day per contact, in person, never for more pay, XP or rep; drop (a second right-click) only a taken job, after arrivals are settled. A job whose heist is deleted is replaced at once; disabled, out of reach or missing what the template needs (a safe, a vault, advanced): replaced after `jobs::grace` (15 min).

### Hit contracts (hits.sk, built 2026-09-27)
- One wanted target a week for everyone (core.sk `hit::targets`: a name, a crime, an NPC name with a space in it so Citizens never shows a real account's skin; skins optional). Contracts are taken in person from **the Broker** (a quest giver of the kind "hits", "Pool Hall"): Easy (level 15, $10,000, 100 XP), Medium (level 30, $25,000, 250 XP), Hard (level 50, $60,000, 600 XP, a Season key), PROPOSAL; one a week, switching only to an easier one until the takedown.
- Windows, shared: Easy 14 a week (2 a day), Medium 7, Hard 5 (5 of the 7 days), random start times rolled when the week turns. A window runs only while its tier is active (2+ online, or a holder of that tier online); one that comes due while it isn't waits (only the latest; older ones are dropped); its hour counts only active time; a fight going on when it ends gets `hit::overtime` (5 min).
- The hunt: the target walks staff street nodes (`/dhit node add|addat|link|route on`, never near heists or safe zones) as a memory-only walker; tips from the Broker put a search area on the GPS (the quest slot, "hit") that shrinks while the hunter keeps up; within `hit::materialize` (64) blocks the hunter's own copy appears: the target and 2 bodyguards (Citizens PLAYER NPCs + Sentinel, made one at a time with the cops' lock, core.sk `npcBusy`), shown only to the hunter (`npc playerfilter -a --add <uuid>` before the spawn: no other client ever gets them). In sight for a second = SPOTTED (glow, a red locator dot for the hunter only: DonatingPhone's `/dphone mark`); close for 2 s or hit = noticed: the target runs (Sentinel avoid) and the bodyguards shoot (Sentinel melee with a 16-block reach: no projectile anyone else could see or be hit by; the crack and a tracer go to the hunter only). Sentinel sets every bodyguard hit to its `damage` (2.5, difficulty 2's cop) and its safeshot cancels hits on anyone who isn't a target yet. 64+ blocks away for 20 s = lost (a tip again).
- Only the hunter hurts the copy and only the copy hurts the hunter (never in a safe zone). A bodyguard's kill is the "hit" death cause (`hit::death-p` 3%, capped at the bag like a death outside a heist) and a "hit" combat tag. The takedown pays at once (never boosted, no season points): the tier's pay × (1 + 0.05 × weeks in a row, at most +20%; one missed week forgiven) × 0.75 passive, the XP, the Hard key; bound titles Cleaner, Professional, The Reaper (3, 6, 10 weeks in a row) and Hitman (5 Hard takedowns). progress.sk counts life::hit.
- Cleanup: the copy goes when the hunter quits, dies, goes AFK or enters a heist (unless the copy fought them in the last 30 s: no free reset mid-fight; /afk is blocked in combat), is 96+ blocks away for 10 s, or the target can't be found for 3 s (its chunk unloaded); the walker goes on from the last spot. A spawn still waiting on the lock when its hunt ends removes what it made (a token). The week turning mid-hunt ends it (copy, walker, search area); the takedown only pays while the target is in town (or in overtime). Carrying loot or on a car job, the search area gives way to that route and comes back after. Copies are never made inside heists or safe zones; a full city (hit::cap) tells the hunter. No death messages for the hunt: the target's, the bodyguards' and a hunter's killed by a bodyguard are deleted (flagged at the lowest priority: Citizens drops the NPC metadata at LOW and the copy is removed at normal priority); no kill effect on a target. Every 10 s a sweep removes hit NPCs no copy owns (saved ids too, for unloaded chunks) and untracked NPCs with a target's or bodyguard's name; saved ids are removed on load; lookups only scan 128 blocks around the holder.
- Bounties are posted in person with the Broker (bounty.sk `bountyPostBlock`: away from him the GPS leads there and nothing is charged; a map with no Broker keeps posting anywhere); /bounty alone lists them anywhere.
- Since 2026-10-21 the week and the days are the game clock's ("The city clock"): a new target every game week (140 real minutes), sightings per game day for 6 game hours (5 real minutes of active time), a streak up to 8 game weeks apart.
- Players: /hits (/hit; the GPS's Quests page, whose "◀ Back" it has), the sidebar line `%donating_hit%`, the HIT boss bar. Staff: /dhit node ...|route|week [list]|info|end (donating.staff; `end` closes the player's contract for the week, no pay), give|reset|window <tier> open|close|week reroll (donating.quests.admin; a reroll marks windows already past as used). Log: hits.log. Verified with bots (bots\run.js hits, 35 checks: only the hunter's client gets the NPCs; damage gates, also against a player the bodyguard's Sentinel would shoot; noticed by a hit alone; SPOTTED; the takedown pays once with a forgiven streak; no death messages; the "hit" death costs 3% capped at the bag; AFK mid-fight; an unloaded target; the week turning; the schedule (14/7/5, Hard on 5 days, a due window waits while nobody can play and only the latest opens); the active rule; bounties at the Broker).
