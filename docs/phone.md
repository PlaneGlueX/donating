# The phone plugin and phone menus

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-03. Grep a heading, then read that part: don't read the whole file.

### Phone plugin (DonatingPhone, built 2026-09-25)
- Source: `plugin\src\dev\donating\phone\PhonePlugin.java` (one class, about 400 lines) + `plugin\resources\plugin.yml` and `config.yml`. Build with `tools\build-plugin.ps1` (plain javac + jar against `server\libraries`, no Gradle), then restart. The jar isn't in git (`*.jar`); rebuild it from source.
- It only draws. Skript owns every rule and every item. The interface:
  - Plugin → Skript: player metadata `donating_phone_map` = the player's phone map id (set on join at LOWEST, before Skript). inventory.sk's `phoneItem(p)` puts it on the phone. Without the plugin the phone has no map id and shows nothing (fails closed: no positions leak).
  - Skript → plugin: scoreboard tags `donating_passive` (nav.sk) and `donating_phone_open` (phone.sk).
  - `/dphone` (op): reload the config and re-read the city (after adding banner labels). `/dphone status <player>`: one line for tests (map id, open, labels, cursor).
- The city (config `city-maps`): one locked map, or a grid of locked maps of the same scale side by side, like a map wall (`[[12, 13], [14, 15]]`, rows north to south). The plugin reads them into one image (plus their banner labels) and checks they line up. A big city stays sharp this way: e.g. 3x3 scale-1 maps = 768x768 blocks at 2 blocks per pixel.
- Held phone: a north-up GPS view centered on you, the city zoomed 2x (`zoom`), re-centered once you're 4 screen pixels from the middle (`follow-step`), with a hint strip "R-click: map  F: apps". Shown one-handed next to the bag. Passive players are green arrows with no names (owner, 2026-09-25).
- Right-click (phone.sk): the big map. The whole city is shrunk to fit the screen: f = max(W, H) / 128 city pixels per screen pixel, W x H = the city image. phone.sk adds the open tag and takes the bag out of the offhand (a map only shows big and centered with an empty offhand); the plugin tilts the camera to 70° once (`open-pitch`, 0 = off; it aims at a point 1000 blocks away, because the client turns from its own eye position, which is ahead of the server's when moving), because a two-handed map only faces the camera at 49.5° or more (26.3 client). Right-click again, a slot change, F, a hit that lands (phone.sk, 2026-09-29: "Hit: your map closed." on the action bar; the tilted camera and the missing bag left no way to fight back), death (applyLayout on respawn) and quitting close it and put the bag back.
- Big map cursor ("hover"): a held map can't be hovered with the mouse, so turning your head moves a white cursor drawn on the map (`cursor-speed` 3 screen pixels per degree; at the edge it stays put like a mouse pointer). A passive player's name shows in green only while the cursor is within `hover-radius` (6) pixels of their arrow (owner's request). The cursor starts in the middle once the client reports the tilted camera (`Player#lookAt` also sets the server's copy of the rotation at once, so the plugin waits for a PlayerMoveEvent near the target pitch, 1 s at most). Pixels go out every tick, names 4 times a second (Paper sends a custom map's icons every 5th update).
- Arrows on the big map are 2/3 size (`small-arrows`): the plugin sends the unused icon types jungle_temple (you) and swamp_hut (passive), and the pack redraws them as small white and green arrows. Without the pack they'd look like a temple and a hut: set `small-arrows: false` then. Icon positions always follow the zoom.
- F with the phone: the apps menu (phone.sk `openPhone`, a phone-style home screen since 2026-09-29: see "Phone-style menus (ui.sk)"). The 26.3 client doesn't swap hands locally, so cancelling F doesn't flicker.
- Phone out, no block use (owner, 2026-09-27): right-clicking a button, door, lever, container or similar (phone.sk phoneUsable, by the block's name) with the phone in hand doesn't use it and doesn't toggle the map; a quiet gray action-bar hint says "Put your phone away to use that" (phoneHint, at most once a second, also swallowing the click's second half). Loot and safe/vault hitboxes the same ("to grab loot", loot.sk). A plain block or the air still toggles the map; NPCs, cars, crate stands and garage bays keep working.
- Nametags only in the open (owner, 2026-09-27: "hide nametags for players behind walls, keep them if out in the open"): DonatingPhone's Nametags.java checks every 5 ticks, for players within 64 blocks, three rays from the viewer's eyes to the other's head, chest and legs; only full opaque blocks stop a ray (glass, leaves, bars, slabs don't). Any clear ray shows the name at once; 2 checks in a row with none hide it. TAB draws the names (its teams sort the tab list), so it goes through TAB's per-viewer API (hideNameTag(player, viewer); TAB 6.2, softdepend, compiled against plugins\TAB-*.jar by build-plugin.ps1). Config `nametags.enabled|range|every|hide-after`; `/dphone nametag <viewer> <target>` for tests. bots\run.js nametags (7 checks: both ways, glass, a waist-high wall, TAB's "never"/"always" team updates in the client).
- Icons: you (white arrow), passive players (green arrow; players hidden from the viewer with `Player#canSee` stay off), and the city's banner labels. Nobody else, ever.
- One map id per online player, from a pool of 16 maps the plugin creates on first start (ids in `plugins/DonatingPhone/pool.yml`, so the plugin never rewrites config.yml). Reason: Paper's `CraftMapCanvas.setPixel` marks pixels dirty for every player carrying that map id, so a shared id would send everyone's scrolling to everyone. More than 16 online: players share (still correct, more traffic).
- Cost: nothing is drawn while the phone is pocketed; a full redraw (16,384 pixels) only on a re-center or view change; the cursor is 17 pixels. Traffic per player holding the phone: T = (v / d) × 16 KB, with d = follow-step × bpp / zoom blocks (bpp = blocks per city-map pixel). Scale-2 city, driving at 25 blocks/s: about 50 KB/s before compression. Raise `follow-step` if that matters. City image: W × H bytes (3x3 maps = 147 KB).
- Phone icon (owner, 2026-09-25): the phone looks like a phone in the inventory, on the ground and in other players' hands. inventory.sk's `phoneItem` sets custom_model_data strings `["donating:phone"]`; the pack's `assets/minecraft/items/filled_map.json` selects `donating:item/phone` for it and the vanilla map model for every other map (without the pack the phone looks like a map). Held in first person it's still the map.
- Cars in the pack (2026-09-26, owner: merge and host it like WeaponMechanics'): build-pack.js also merges MTVehicles' pack (extras\packs\MTVehicles_Pack_v0.2.3_1.21.4.zip, SHA-256 in downloads.json): only the cars vehicles.yml lists (items/diamond_hoe.json's damage dispatch filtered), their models and textures (window textures fixed from pre-1.13 paths, particle slots set), textures/custom/ added to the block atlas with our own atlas file, the configured horn, and MTVehicles-credits.txt. sounds.json is joined with WeaponMechanics' event by event; any other clash stops the build. The pack is 2.96 MB and the block atlas stayed 4096x4096 (checked in the client log).
- Server pack build (2026-09-25): `tools\build-pack.js` merges WeaponMechanics' official pack (`extras\packs\wm\WeaponMechanicsResourcePack-3.0.0.zip`, fetched by `tools\fetch.ps1` with its hash; the owner approved it) with our `pack\` on top into `extras\packs\Donating-pack.zip`. WM's README allows merging it into a server pack and hosting it for our players; it forbids selling it, claiming it as ours, or publishing it in packs/plugins online, so it stays out of git and its README ships in the zip as `WeaponMechanics-README.yml` (credits: Darkman_Bree, Rosstail, Dotoo, Stephen_GFX). Until 2026-09-29 it gave every sold weapon its 3D model through WM's skin numbers on the feather (Uzi 1, AK-47 5, .50 GS 9, R9-0 14, +1000 scoped, +2000 sprinting; knife -10; Stim -1); now our own models draw those numbers (see "Gun models" below) and WM's pack still gives the unsold guns, gun sounds and the crosshair.
- Item art (`tools\make-item-art.js`, 2026-09-25): the bag per tier (a 16x16 icon in inventories via `display_context` "gui", a 3D duffel everywhere else: carried by its handle in the offhand, a duffel on the ground), ammo icons (light, shells, rifle), the XP bar as a brass ammo belt, and the tab-list glyphs (U+E000 logo, U+E001 coin, U+E002 skull, U+E003 person, U+E004 ping) added to the default font next to vanilla's references. Items pick their art by the first custom_model_data string (`donating:bag_<tier>`, `donating:ammo_<type>`, `donating:phone`: inventory.sk's bagItem/phoneItem, shop.sk's ammoItem); anything else keeps the vanilla look. Bitmap glyphs take the text color, so put `&f` before them. Tuning a model: change it in the script, rebuild, `/zzpack <player>` resends the pack without rejoining.
- Loot and tool art (2026-09-25, same script): 16x16 sprites for the loot pieces (cash stack on paper, jewel on diamond, gold bar on gold_ingot, a watch for valuables on emerald, a golden idol for artifacts on gold_block), the loot marker (gold_nugget, next to the rifle ammo) and the heist tools (Drill on iron_ingot, Safe Kit on flint; 3D box models since 2026-10-13, the owner: "make models for the case and drill in the shop", shown 3D in menus and the hotbar too: the Drill a yellow cordless drill whose bit points into the vault door loot.sk mounts it on (an item display, no display transform), the Safe Kit a hard case with a code dial, carried by its handle), picked by `donating:loot_<style>`, `donating:marker`, `donating:tool_<t>`. The script now collects every case per base item and writes each `items/<base>.json` once (WeaponMechanics' pack only overrides feather.json). A clock can't be a base item: its vanilla definition is a time-based dispatch our select would have to copy.
- Bullet and duffel art (2026-09-26, same script): the tracers `donating:item/tracer_<light|rifle|pellet>`, picked by `minecraft/items/iron_nugget.json` from custom model data 7001-7003 (a range_dispatch; see Bullets in the WeaponMechanics notes), and the dropped loot duffel `donating:bag_loot` in leather.json (the bag duffel's shape in canvas with a green band and a gold $). The script writes files with split/join: in JavaScript's `String.replace`, `$$` in the replacement becomes `$` (it drew the $ as a zigzag once).
- Gear art (owner, 2026-09-25: "tactical gear art"): Light Helmet (grey), Tactical Helmet (olive with goggles), Light Vest (navy), Heavy Vest (black plate carrier, olive pouches), each an inventory icon (`donating:gear_<id>` on iron_helmet, diamond_helmet, chainmail_chestplate, diamond_chestplate) and a worn look: shop.sk's `gearItem(id)` gives the item an equippable component with asset id `donating:<helmet|vest>_<n>` (Skript's experimental syntax: `using equippable components` at the top of shop.sk; `a blank equippable component`, `equipped model id of`, `equipment slot of`, `equippable component of`), and the pack has `assets/donating/equipment/<asset>.json` + `textures/entity/equipment/humanoid/<asset>.png` (the 64x32 armor layout; transparent keeps the face and arms visible). The base item keeps its armor points (tested: a Tactical Helmet still gives 3). SkBee NBT couldn't set the equippable component (the item's NBT stayed empty).
- Shop icons look like the real items (owner, 2026-09-25: "its all feathers right now"): shop.sk's `wpnIcon(W)` is a feather with the weapon's WeaponMechanics skin as its custom_model_data float (core.sk `wpn::<W>::model` / `con::<W>::model`: Uzi 1, AK-47 5, .50 GS 9, R9-0 14, knife -10, Stim -1, from `Skin: Default` in each weapon file), `ammoIcon(type)` and `bagIcon(t)` carry the ammo and bag model strings; the hotbar mirror shows what's really in each slot. A new gun needs its `model` number too.
- Resource pack: `pack\` is Donating's own pack source, drawn by `tools\make-phone-art.js`: the phone frame replaces `textures/map/map_background*.png` (a dark bezel with side buttons, and a dark screen that shows where the plugin draws color 0, outside the city), and the two small arrows replace `textures/map/decorations/jungle_temple.png` and `swamp_hut.png`. Without the pack the map has the vanilla paper frame and everything else still works.
- Rules for other scripts: never set the offhand directly while `donating_phone_open` is set; use `applyBag()` (inventory.sk) and `closePhoneMap()` (phone.sk). garage.sk calls `closePhoneMap()` before turning slot 8 into the car key. death.sk: the respawn's `applyLayout` already closes the map.
- Minecraft's own crosshair stays in the middle of the big map (the client draws it; hiding it needs a pack change that would also hide it in fights).
- Places on the map (2026-09-28): every POI also shows on the phone's map as a colored banner (base light blue, shop orange, spawn white, landmark magenta, garage cyan, quest givers blue, heists by difficulty: a store light gray, then lime, yellow, red, black), on the held phone and the big map; its name shows on the big map while the cursor is on it (a left-click there pins it: gps.sk snaps to POIs). nav.sk's poiMapReconcile (every 10 s, with the locator-bar POIs) sends `/dphone place set <id> <type> <world> <x> <z> <name>` when one changes and `remove` when it's gone (ids p<n>, h<heist>, q<n>), and everything again every minute (the plugin keeps them in memory). Heists only while open or running. Config `places.enabled`, `places.held`. A place is drawn at direction 8, like vanilla's banners (0 hangs it upside down: review fix, 2026-09-29). bots\run.js places.
- Wall maps (`WallMaps.java`, 2026-09-29; the idea "a big wall map at the base"): `/dphone wall create <name> <cols> <rows>` (1-8 a side), looking at the wall's bottom-left block (a north, south, east or west face; console: `create <name> <cols> <rows> <world> <x> <y> <z> <face>`), hangs cols x rows invisible, fixed, invulnerable glow item frames (tags `donating_wall`, `wall_<name>`), each with a map of its own, that together show the whole city (the phone's city image, the scan or the city-maps, scaled to fit and centered; outside it dark), every place (nav.sk's POIs, open heists, quest givers) as its banner with its name always shown, and a green "You are here" arrow in front of the wall. Same picture for everyone (no players); the maps are redrawn when the city changes, the icons when the places change (and every second). Refused: no solid block behind a frame, a frame or painting already covering a tile's slot (the 1/16-block slab against the wall), a name that exists. A live wall's frames can't be broken or damaged (HangingBreakEvent and damage cancelled: creative clicks, the block behind going); `remove <name>` takes the frames down (a frame of a removed wall left in an unloaded chunk: break it in creative) and its map ids are used again by the next wall when every frame went; `list` shows frames=<present>/<loaded>, `repair <name>` hangs missing frames again with their own maps (a kill command lost them; wm-reload.js's kill-all skips `donating_wall` now). Places follow `places.enabled`; the city maps' own banner labels show too; an icon in a tile's last quarter pixel is clamped, not lost; a wall map id is never read as a city map. Saved in `plugins/DonatingPhone/walls.yml` (the frames and `world/data/minecraft/maps/<id>.dat` travel with the world; without the plugin the maps show nothing). bots\run.js walls (13 checks: the frames, a second wall on the same spot refused, the picture pixel for pixel against the city maps (2x2) and shrunk (1x1), the arrow, a named place coming and going, refusals, a reload, the block behind broken, a killed frame repaired, removing, reusing the maps; walls.js and cityscan.js put the owner's city.yml/city.bin back). Reviewed (2 lenses + a skeptic each, 2026-09-29): 7 confirmed, all fixed.
- Review of the city scan (3 lenses + a skeptic each, 2026-09-29, 11 confirmed with the districts ones, all fixed): roads.bin keeps its cells when the city is drawn at another scale (Roads.fits checks k, Gps.city redraws the same cells at the new pixels per cell; before, the route was drawn in the wrong place), a scan over 40,000 chunks needs `confirm` at the end and the chunks are counted, not queued (a typo made a 120 MB queue), city.bin and roads.bin are written to a .tmp on the worker and moved into place in one step on the main thread only while the scan still counts (a cancel during saving replaced them; a failed rename lost them), the automatic scale works on the lined-up pixel sizes, a tie goes to the color first seen x-outer like vanilla, no depth walk under the bottom block, `/dphone city use` during a scan drops the scan (nothing written: the choice stays, on disk too), `use scan` refuses a city.bin of an unloaded world, a city change that leaves the grid as it was doesn't cancel a running road scan, and the cityscan test puts a real city.bin aside and back.
- Another world's own city and the big map's zoom (2026-10-14; the owner: "update the GPS map on the phone (try to load the whole island then allow the player to zoom in on their location for more specific details)"): the main city stays as it is (city-maps or city.bin, one world: the test city), and a scan of any other world is that world's own city, `plugins/DonatingPhone/cities/<world>.bin` (`/dphone city scan <x1> <z1> <x2> <z2> [scale] [world]`: a world name at the end, or the world a player stands in; `city remove <world>`, `city info` lists `others=`, `city pixel <x> <z> [world]`). A player sees their world's own city, else the main one (PhonePlugin.City: world, corner, bpp, size, pixels; WallMaps and the road grid stay on the main city). The prison: `/dphone city scan -40 -30 480 470 0 jailbreak` (the build's bounds at 1 block a pixel: 521x501 px, ~1,050 chunks, about a minute). Zoom: sneak with the big map open (not in a vehicle: sneaking gets out; not on another world's map) cycles config `big-zoom` [1, 2, 4, 8]: f = max(0.25, fit / z) city pixels a screen pixel (fit = the whole city), levels that would look the same are left out; above 1 the view is around the player, re-centered every `follow-step` screen pixels, clamped to the city's edge; the cursor, places, hover names, pins (a left-click picks at the zoomed spot) and the route follow the drawn view (State.vc/vcx/vcz/vf); the strip says "L: pin  Shift: x4"; the zoom is kept for the session. The held phone is unchanged. `/dphone zoom <player> [level]`; `/dphone status` adds `shown=main:<world>|own:<world> zoom= view=<cx>,<cz>,<f>,big|held`. The map is drawn from above: inside buildings it shows the roof.
- The city scan (`CityScan.java`, 2026-09-29; the idea "a staff command that paints city maps without flying over them"): `/dphone city scan [x1 z1 x2 z2] [scale 0-4]` draws the phone's city image from the world exactly the way vanilla fills a map (1.21.11 MapItem.update: each pixel is the most common map color of its blocks' tops (walking down through blocks with no map color, like glass), its height the average; shaded bright/normal/dark by the height difference to the pixel north of it, (d1 - d0) × 4 / (bpp + 4) ± 0.2 in a checker against ±0.6; water by its depth, depth × 0.1 + 0.2 on odd pixels against 0.5 / 0.9). It matches the local vanilla city maps on 100.00% of the colors and 99.99% of the exact pixels. No box: the city the phones show now (maps or the last scan) at its scale (a new scale: `scan <scale>`). Default scale 1 (2 blocks a pixel, like the recommended scale-1 maps), higher only when a side would pass 2,048 pixels; pixels line up with multiples of their size (so each lies in one chunk). Like the road scan: at most 1 new chunk a tick, 4 at a time, none while ticks run over 40 ms, never generating land (a missing chunk stays see-through), the work on its own thread; about 15-20 chunks a second (a 768x768-block city: ~2,300 chunks, ~2.5 min). When it's done it saves `plugins/DonatingPhone/city.bin` (gzip: world, corner, blocks a pixel, size, the palette bytes) and `city.yml` (`source: scan`, the plugin's own file like pool.yml), and the phones use it at once (labels come from the places, not banners). `/dphone city use scan|maps` switches (the maps are config.yml's city-maps), `info`, `cancel`, `pixel <x> <z>` (tests). A new city box means the GPS's roads.bin no longer fits: the reply says to run `/dphone roads scan` (switching back to the old box finds roads.bin again). Upload city.bin and city.yml with the plugin folder, or run the scan on Minehut (it only reads loaded or saved chunks). bots\run.js cityscan (12 checks).
- Locator-bar icons (2026-10-03; the idea "a GPS dot icon of our own"): every POI kind, heists, quest givers, the GPS dot and the hunter's mark have an icon on the locator bar instead of the same dot: a house (base), a shopping bag (shop), a star (spawn), a diamond (landmark), a parking P (garage), an exclamation mark (quest givers), a safe (every heist, in its difficulty's color), a map pin (the GPS dot), a target ring (`/dphone mark`); beyond 332 blocks every kind is a small dot in its color (the server sends a waypoint past 332 as a direction only, at an infinite distance for the client). `tools\node\node.exe tools\make-waypoint-art.js` draws them (9x9 like vanilla's: a light icon with a dark 4-neighbour outline, the waypoint color tinting the light pixels) into `pack\assets\donating\waypoint_style\<id>.json` and `textures\gui\sprites\hud\locator_bar_dot\<id>.png` (checked in the 26.3 client jar: WaypointStyle reads near_distance, far_distance and sprites, picks the first sprite under near, the last at or past far, else the middle of three; sprites live under hud/locator_bar_dot/ in the GUI atlas). nav.sk sets `waypoint modify <stand> style set donating:<kind>` (core.sk `poi::<kind>::style`, "default" = vanilla's dot; the state cache includes it); DonatingPhone `gps.style` (default donating:gps) and Marks' donating:target through Paper's LivingEntity#setWaypointStyle. A client without the pack draws an unknown style as the missing texture (26.3 WaypointStyleManager's MISSING, no fallback to the dot): Minehut's pack is required (DEPLOY.md); locally the pack server serves it. bots\run.js poi checks the style in the track packet (the shop) and on a heist's stand.

### Phone-style menus (ui.sk, tools\make-phone-ui.js, 2026-09-29)
- Owner, 2026-09-29: "make a phone style apps menu (its very cluttered right now), most people can use context clues, so get rid of obvious explanations on everything". A chest menu's title can draw a picture: the 26.3 client draws a container's title after its background texture and before its items (checked in the client with a test glyph: the items sit on top, the slots line up exactly). The pack's font `donating:phone_ui` (referenced from minecraft's default.json, written by make-item-art.js) maps U+E010 (the home screen) and U+E011 (an app page) to 176x135 bitmaps (ascent 13), and U+F801-F808 / U+F811-F818 to spaces of -1, -2, -4 ... -128 / +1 ... +128 pixels. ui.sk's `phoneTitle(kind, status)` = "&f" + 8 left + the background + 132 left + "&f" + the status text (x 45, the phone's status bar); `uiSpace(n)` builds any shift; the characters come from code points (`character from codepoint N`: the Edit/Write tools and some editors silently drop private-use characters, so never type them into a script). Without the pack the title is a few boxes and the menu still works.
- Art: `tools\node\node.exe tools\make-phone-ui.js [--preview <dir>]` draws both backgrounds (tools\phoneui\bg.js: a dark phone body over the vanilla panel colors, a status bar with signal and battery, a home button ring around slot 49; the home screen has a navy-to-purple wallpaper and a dock row, an app page a dark screen with a toolbar row) and 40 app icons (tools\phoneui\icons-apps.js, icons-gps.js, shared helpers in common.js: 16x16 rounded squares with a gradient and a white symbol, iOS-like colors) into pack\assets\donating (font, textures/font, textures/item/app, models/item/app) and minecraft/items/light_gray_dye.json. An app icon is a light gray dye with custom model data "donating:app_<name>" (ui.sk `appIcon(name, display)`; "blank" is see-through, for the home button). Run it before build-pack.js. Drawn and critiqued by a workflow (3 artists, 2 critique rounds), procedurally: no vanilla texture is copied (the panel border colors were read from generic_54 to match).
- The phone's home screen (phone.sk openPhone, 6 rows; the screen is columns 2-6): row 0 Crates (keys, a glint and "Daily key ready"), Cosmetics, Season (your line), Bounties (yours), your head (level, balance, bounty, bag, play time, since); row 1 Passive (on/off, one line on what it does), How to play; the dock (row 4) Messages (the unread count as the stack size), GPS, Missions (the mission line), Garage, Bag ($ / capacity); slot 49 the home button (Close). Only live state is shown; each app is its name. Clicks go by the item's id ("phone:<app>"), not the slot. The title's status text is the world's time. Contracts, Jobs and Hits aren't apps any more: they're on the GPS's Quests page.
- The GPS app (gps.sk `gpsMenu`, an app page "GPS"): 2 ◀ Phone, 4 the route now (a pin: click removes it; the quest or the base), 5/6 a target on hold (click: switch to it), 11 Quests, 12 Heists ("N open"), 13 Shops, 14 Places, 15 your car (the garage's find), 49 Home. A category (`gpsCat(p, cat, page)`, "GPS · Quests" ...): up to 20 entries in columns 2-6 of rows 1-4, sorted by distance (Quests keep their order), ◀/▶ pages at 5/6, ◀ GPS at 2. **Quests**: Missions (Mara), Car contracts, Side jobs, Wanted List (Vic), Hit contracts, Bounties, each with its state and the nearest giver ("Scrap Yard Boss · 140m"); a click routes the GPS (the tracked mission, a taken job, a hit's search area) or pins the giver, a right-click opens its menu (whose ◀ Back comes back to Quests). **Heists**: every heist not disabled, at its exit spot (its icon by difficulty, the hologram's status, a level lock, the distance). **Shops**: the nearest shopkeeper of each kind, and shop POIs with no keeper within 16 blocks. **Places**: every other POI (base, garages, spawn, landmarks) and the nearest stand of each crate.
