### MTVehicles (checked in its 2.5.9 bytecode and in the real 26.3 client, 2026-09-26)
- It fires its vehicle-enter event before checking ownership (drivers and passengers), and Skript can cancel it (`on mtv vehicle enter`: event-player, event-text = the plate, event-location). There's no seat value: a click on a car that has a driver is a passenger seat (garage.sk tracks the driver).
- `set vehicle owner of {_car} to player` changes MTVehicles' memory at once, but vehicleData.yml is only written every 10 minutes, on stop, or on a delete: a crash can lose new cars. garage.sk keeps its own record and makes a lost plate again (carEnsure).
- `spawn mtv vehicle with license plate X at loc` has no duplicate check (garage.sk and the sweep prevent doubles); `despawn mtv vehicle` removes only stands in loaded chunks; `delete mtv vehicle` also removes the data. `a new mtv vehicle with uuid <model> and owner <player>` makes a plate and nothing else (evaluate it once). The mtvehicle type can't be saved in variables: keep plates as text.
- A car is invisible armor stands named MTVEHICLES_SKIN|MAIN|MAINSEAT|SEAT<n>_<plate>; only those small stands are clickable, not the whole model. They're not protected: garage.sk cancels damage to them (two hits broke one and dropped the car item).
- Driving only uses WASD + jump (horn) and needs no item in hand; it works with a 26.3 client through ViaVersion (checked). Right-clicking a car with a gun in hand doesn't fire it. Never use a diamond hoe for custom items (it's MTVehicles' car item); car icons in menus are diamond hoes with the model's damage and unbreakable.
- WorldGuard's region events do fire for a driver: pvp.sk's safe-zone gate threw a combat-tagged driver out at the border (checked in the client). MTVehicles' own `on mtv vehicle region enter` (cancellable, ejects) only runs on driving input, so a teleported car doesn't trigger it: garage.sk also tows a car that ends up in a heist.
- Settings (plugins/MTVehicles/config.yml): `autoUpdate: false` (it would download updates), `carPickup: true` (true = pickup disabled), `trunkEnabled: false`, `fuelEnabled: false`, `gasStations.enabled: false`, damage off. vehicles.yml is trimmed to Donating's 24 variants (sedan, sport sedan, jeep, sports car, hotrod, SUV, the 3 crate cars) with fuel and trunks off per model; never change a model's itemDamage or remove a variant once cars of it exist (a car finds its model by its look). messagesLanguage is en (supersecretsettings.yml).
- Permissions: plugin.yml declares none, so every mtvehicles.* check falls back to op, and LuckPerms' `*` grants them: the owner group has `mtvehicles.ride` and `mtvehicles.oppakken` set false (redo on Minehut). Players' /vehicle and aliases are blocked by garage.sk (staff only: its menus hand out car items).
- NPC cars need an owner account that has joined the server once, or MTVehicles refuses entry.
- Each car is 3+ armor stands, so the despawn rules matter on 1 GB RAM.
- Skript's list-variable indexes are lowercased (`case-insensitive variables`): a plate read back from loop-index must be uppercased before any MTVehicles call (found 2026-09-26: idle despawns silently failed).

### Cars (garage.sk, car-cleanup.sk, built 2026-09-26)
- Owner, 2026-09-26: cars are a mid-game goal (no starter car; the cheapest $40,000 at level 2, the best $1M at level 6) and the getaway: allowed while wanted, but the cops' chase radius doubles in a car (heists.sk heistInRange, `alarm::car-chase-factor` 2). The model list is a PROPOSAL (the owner skipped that question): Sedan $40K L20 (5 colors), Sport Sedan $90K L35 (3), Jeep $150K L50 (2), Sports Car $250K L75 (5), Hotrod $450K L100 (3), SUV $1M L150 (3) (levels on the 2026-09-27 scale); the crate cars Vandal (a diamond sports car), Specter (a rainbow sport sedan) and Overclock (a gold hotrod) only from crates, no level, looks only (they drive like their family). core.sk `cars::*`, `car::<id>::name|price|level|crate`, `carcolors::<id>::*` = "Label=MTV uuid=damage".
- Buying: the Car Dealer (a shopkeeper: `/dshopkeeper add cars`, Citizens: `dshop open <p> cars`): a model, then a color, a second click within 5 s confirms (the first turns the button into a green "Confirm purchase?" block with the price, like the shops; the same for repaints and tuning since 2026-09-29, which only said so in chat, hidden while a menu is open); several of a model since 2026-09-28 (up to `car::garage-max` 60 cars in all); the level from levels.sk (and /levels lists the cars). Repainting in the garage: $2,500 (`car::paint-price`), a new plate in the new color (MTVehicles finds a car's model by its look), the old car deleted; only while it's in the garage.
- /garage (alias /cars; the phone's Garage app): left-click calls a car next to you (a clear spot ahead, right, left or behind; not in heists; not while in combat; once per `car::call-cooldown` 20 s); one car out per player (calling another sends the first back); right-click: lock/unlock, repaint, send back. Staff: `/dgarage give|take|info|store <player> [car] [color]`.
- Getting in: only the owner drives (MTVehicles checks too; owners never get riders). Locked (the default): nobody else gets in. Unlocked: others ride as passengers (added as MTVehicles members, removed on lock and when the car goes back). No getting in from inside a heist. While driving, slot 9 is the car key (tripwire hook tagged `carkey:<plate>`: right-click locked/unlocked until 2026-10-21; since then F in your own car's driver's seat locks/unlocks whatever is in hand (garage.sk `on swap hand items` at the lowest priority, before WeaponMechanics; a stolen car has no lock) and the key's right-click toggles the phone's big map while driving (phone.sk openPhoneMap; DonatingPhone CarCam pauses the chase view meanwhile, the slot to put back kept for the ride's end); no locking in regions starting `car::nolock-prefix` "nolock"); the phone comes back when you get out (MTVehicles' leave event, plus an every-second check for teleports, deaths, quits).
- car-cleanup.sk: a car goes back to the garage after `car::idle-despawn` (5 min) unused, when its owner drives another car for `car::other-car-despawn` (1 min: driving, not riding along), and when its owner logs out; sending a car back loads its chunk first. Every 10 s a sweep removes loaded stands one by one (the vanilla kill command, helmet cleared first: no car item drops): any stand of ours whose car isn't out or that's more than 8 blocks from the car's tracked spot (the driver, else where it's parked), and any stand of a plate MTVehicles doesn't know; it forgets a car that's out but whose stands are gone. Getting into such a stray copy is refused ("This car was towed"). Review, 2026-09-26: MTVehicles' despawn takes every copy of a plate, also the one being driven, so the sweep never uses it.
- Safe zones and cars (review, 2026-09-26): combat-tagged players can't get into a car parked in a safe zone; a car isn't let into a safe zone when its driver or any passenger is tagged (MTVehicles' region event; passengers fire no region events of their own); and every second anyone in a car who is tagged inside a safe zone is put out and back to where they last were outside one.
- The chase radius (heists.sk `heistChaseRadius`, also the boss bar's "blocks to lose them"): doubled once the hunted robber has been in a car during that chase, until they're released (stepping out at 150 blocks doesn't lose the cops).
- Crate cars: crates.sk's car lines call `garageGrantCar` (the car lands in the garage; a repeat pays the crate's dupe value); `crate::cars` is true and a car line only rolls if the car exists.
- Reviewed (3 reviewers + a skeptic, 2026-09-26): 13 confirmed, all fixed (the stray copies, safe zones by car, the sticky chase radius, the other-car rule, crate car icons, missing tests).
- Verified with bots (bots\run.js garage) and in the real client (PLAYTEST 110): the dealer (level, confirm, charge, one per model), calling, only the owner drives, locked/unlocked passengers, the key, the phone back, unbreakable stands, /vehicle blocked, no cars in heists (getting in, and a car towed out), the chase radius in a car, idle and logout despawns, repainting, crate cars, /levels, staff only.

### Smoother cars and the chase camera (DonatingPhone CarSmooth.java, CarCam.java, carcam.sk; 2026-09-29)
- Owner, 2026-09-29: "research if theres a way for the cars to ride smoother, test it out using bots and my pc, also if possible, make the user go into 3rd person mode automatically when entering the car and back into 1st person when exiting."
- Why cars look choppy (checked in Paper 1.21.11 and the 26.3 client): MTVehicles' car is four invisible armor stands (MAIN physics, SKIN the model, MAINSEAT the driver, SEAT<n> passengers), and armor stands are sent to clients only every 3rd tick (EntityType's updateInterval 3 on both sides; the 26.3 client interpolates them in 3-tick steps), so a car moves on screen in 150 ms steps and a server hitch stalls it. No server config changes that (the configs only have ranges). The research also read two causes out of MTVehicles' bytecode (the seats a tick behind the model; a key change skipping a movement tick): measured, neither happens (seat error 0.000, no skipped tick).
- CarSmooth (a global-region task each tick, after every Bukkit task and before the tracker sends): for every driven car, SKIN, MAINSEAT and SEAT<n> get updateInterval 1 (by reflection on the ServerEntity; set again whenever Paper starts tracking a stand anew) and their ChunkMap.TrackedEntity range raised to `carsmooth.range` 128 (an armor stand is tracked within spigot.yml's 64 while the seat carrying a player gets the player's 128: between 64 and 128 blocks others saw a driver floating with no car). Mode `track` by default; `sync` (snapping seats, refilling skipped ticks) never had anything to fix and stays only as an A/B switch. `/dphone carsmooth off|sync|track|all` (live, kept in plugins\DonatingPhone\carsmooth.yml, which wins over config.yml), `/dphone carprobe <plate> [ticks]` (per-tick rows in carprobe.log). Every reflective step switches only its own part off with one log line. Measured with bots (bots\run.js carsmooth, 11 checks, six runs in a row): off 6.6 moves a second per stand, every gap 150 ms; track 19.9 a second, gaps 50 ms (longest 54-77 ms); about 1.2 KB/s instead of 0.6 per watcher and moving car; no measurable tick cost. Bots drive with their own keys (player_input).
- CarCam, the chase camera (real F5 can't be set by a server: only the key changes it, checked in the 26.3 client): while a player drives (their vehicle is MTVEHICLES_MAINSEAT_<plate>), a camera only they receive (hidden from everyone before it exists; first an item display, now a marker armor stand: next bullets) sits behind the car and the server sends ClientboundSetCameraPacket for it (by reflection; the Paper API only allows spectators). Driving still works (the client sends WASD, jump and sneak in the player-input packet whatever the camera is), sneaking gets out, and every exit path sends the camera back to the player before the display goes (getting out, a teleport, a world change, quitting, death, the phone's big map, /carcam off, errors, plugin disable). While it's on: hotbar slot 9 (the car key; at first its map showed in the corner like a minimap, now a hook: next bullet) is selected, and the player's slot comes back on exit if they're still on 9; a Mannequin wearing the driver's skin rides the seat (visible only to the driver: under a foreign camera the client never draws the local player, so the car looked empty); the hearts, food and hotbar are hidden by the client, so carcam.sk shows health on the action bar ("❤ 9/10 · Sneak: get out · F5 x2: look back"). The seat stand gets camera_distance 7, so F5 sits further back with the chase view off. `/carcam [on|off]` (a player setting, the tag donating_carcam_off); `/dphone cam <player>` (status), `/dphone cam tune <key> <value>` (live). Config carcam.* (distance, height, look-height, yaw-smooth, teleport-duration, only-with-key, select-key, body). The mouse turns the player but not the view (no aiming from a car with the chase view on). bots\run.js carcam (16 checks). Checked in the owner's 26.3 client (2026-09-29): the view behind the car, the car key's map in the corner, the driver in the seat, first person and the old slot back on exit.
- The owner, after driving (2026-09-29): "sometimes, player velocity doesnt match the car and their head glitches out ... the carcam is kinda glitchy, is there any way to make it more like 3rd person f5 mode? like how the camera movement works and also get rid of the hands in the carcam".
