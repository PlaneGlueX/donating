# Shotgun (r90) remake: design spec

Module: `tools\guns\r90.js` (name `gun_r90`; WeaponMechanics `R9_0`, skins Default 14, Scope 1014, Sprint 2014,
Reload 3014). Reference: PG3D's default pump "Shotgun" (`refs\Shotgun\`). Detail target: the Photon Shotgun.

**Recommendation (realistic option):** rebuild it as PG3D's *current* Shotgun (the "Appearance" image, the model
since 10.4.0). That means gunmetal-black tubes and receiver frame with chrome tops and stripes, a bright silver
receiver side plate with a framed port, and a dark-walnut ribbed pump that sits just ahead of the receiver. Use
density 4 and about 75 boxes. Keep our length, grip, sight line and every frame.

**Cautious option:** keep today's all-silver tubes (the 9.x look, closest to what we ship now) and only add the
detail from section 3. That needs no skins.js change.

A working prototype of the realistic option, built with the repo's own pg.js (read only) and rendered with a copy of
render-item.js, is `work\r90\r90proto.js`. Every coordinate below comes from it, and its renders are in
`work\r90\proto-out\`. It is a worked example for the builder, not the build.

Reading the references: the wiki's files are WebP saved under .png/.jpg names. Windows' WIC WebP decoder gets their
alpha wrong, so every crop here was rendered through headless Edge (`work\r90\shot.ps1`, `rotshot.ps1`, `annot.ps1`).

## 1. What the reference looks like, part by part

Files: `refs\Shotgun\01-Shotgun_0.png` (current, 256²), `02-Simple_Shotgun_9_0_0.jpg` (the original model) and
`03-Hd_retexture.jpg` (the previous model). There is no in-use screenshot of this gun. PG3D's first-person framing
comes from `refs\Heavy_Shotgun\02-...png` and `refs\Photon_Shotgun\02-...jpg`: the gun sits big at the bottom right
and points at the crosshair, and the receiver side and top fill the corner.

The wiki says it "appears to be an Akkar Churchill SBS" (also Ithaca Stakeout / Maverick 88). It is a short pump gun
with no stock, a pistol grip, a barrel stacked over a magazine tube, a wood pump on the tube, a small front sight that
can't be used, and a shell port on the right.

Measuring: the icon was turned 31.6° so the barrel is level (`work\r90\ref-app-level-x3.png`, annotated in
`annot-ref-current.png`). It runs 252 px from the receiver's rear (x 14) to the muzzle (x 266). That maps to our 22.9
units (19.9 → −3.0), so 1 px ≈ 0.091 u. The icon is in perspective: the muzzle end is closer to the camera and drawn
about 1.4× the scale of the receiver. Lengths toward the front are therefore overstated and the receiver is
understated. All numbers below are corrected by eye.

| Part | Reference (straightened px) | Reference → our units | Ours now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Receiver (side plate) | x 14–82, plate y 80–112 | ~27% of length (≈ 6.2 u, more after perspective) | 11.5–19.4 (7.9 u, silver box) | core 11.5–18.9 + 2 rear steps to 19.95; plate 11.7–18.6 |
| Receiver vs barrel height | receiver top ~5 px over barrel top | ≈ 0.5 u step | 11.4 vs 11.2 (0.2) | 11.4 vs 10.95 (0.45) |
| Ejection port | x 46–78 (upper 60% of plate) | ≈ 2.9 u | 13.15–16.75 dark recess, small bolt | window 13.25–16.95 × 9.0–10.5, silver bolt 2.1 long inside |
| Pump | x 92–140, right ahead of the receiver | ≈ 4.4 u, ~19% of length | 1.2–7.8 (6.6 u, at the front) | 4.6–9.0 (4.4 u) at rest, 7.0–11.4 racked |
| Clamp (silver bits ahead of pump) | x 146–170 | — | band at −1.5..−0.6 | silver ring 3.3–4.1 |
| Bare front (barrel + tube) | x 140–266 | about half the icon (inflated by perspective) | −3..1.2 | −3.0..4.6 (7.6 u, 33%) |
| Muzzle block | x 238–266, taller than the barrel | ≈ 2.5 u | none | −3.0..−0.6, 0.2–0.3 proud of the barrel |

| Grip | ~34° rake, ≈ 50 px long | ≈ 5.5 u (perspective) | 18°/30°, 7.2 u | 22°/34°, butt 40°, 7.0 u (kept for the hand) |

What the parts look like (current look):

1. **Receiver.** A dark gunmetal frame with a dark top. A bright silver **side plate** covers most of each side; the
   frame shows around it as a dark border. The plate's rear corner is stepped, and the receiver's rear **steps down
   into the grip** (a staircase silhouette, not a square back). Two small dark **pins** sit at the lower rear of the
   plate. The **port** is a black-framed window in the plate's upper front with the silver **bolt** inside.
2. **Barrel.** Black/gunmetal, with a bright **chrome top** and a chrome highlight **band along the upper side**. A dim
   grey streak runs lower on the side. At the front is a black **muzzle block**, taller than the barrel. Its front face
   shows the **bore** as a lit square in a black frame.
3. **Magazine tube.** Under the barrel, the same gunmetal with a chrome stripe. It ends short of the muzzle with a
   lit **end cap**, leaving a notch under the muzzle block.
4. **Pump.** Dark walnut with **transverse ribs** (4–5 lighter ribs and darker grooves) and a lengthwise split, so it
   reads as a grid of pads. The ends are darker. A **silver clamp** sits just ahead of it.
5. **Trigger group.** A black housing under the receiver, a chunky black guard and a small silver trigger.
6. **Grip.** Walnut, strongly raked, with dark grooves or blotches, and a **black butt**.
7. **Front sight.** "A small sight at the end of the barrel". We keep our red bead on the muzzle block.
8. **Older looks** (`ref-orig-level-x2.png`, `ref-prev-level-x4.png`). All silver-white (#ccdbe7 / #fefefe) with a
   slit port and a reddish wood pump at the **front** (≈ 23% of length) over the tube. Our current model is close to
   these.

The Photon Shotgun (`annot-photon-vocabulary.png`) sets the detail level. It has a dark outline on every box, 3 tones
per face with a lit top, rows of studs and rivets, recessed slots, dark mechanical inserts, bright tips and a glowing
panel. For this realistic gun only the vocabulary carries over: screws and pins, panel lines and grooves, a stepped
silhouette, chrome highlights and one small glowing accent.

## 3. Details to add (all built in the prototype)

Coordinates are model units: x from muzzle (−3.0) to tang (19.95), y up, z across (8 is the centre line, +z is the
first-person side, −z the icon side). "Both sides" means mirrored about z = 8 (the prototype's `sides()` helper).
Density is 4 texels a unit (texture 256², 73 boxes in `_full`, 336 faces).

**Receiver** (`body`)
- Core [11.5, 7.0, 6.6]→[18.9, 11.4, 9.4]: `gm`, N/S faces `gmDark` (they show as the plate's dark frame and through
  the port), bottom and rear `gmMid`.
- Rear steps into the grip: [18.9, 8.4, 6.75]→[19.4, 10.5, 9.25] and a tang [19.4, 7.3, 6.9]→[19.95, 9.3, 9.1], with
  `gmHi` tops. The tops read as light lines in the aim view, and they make the silhouette's staircase.
- A tang screw on the rear face [19.95, 8.05, 7.75]→[19.98, 8.55, 8.25] (`silverMid`, seen when aiming).
- Barrel nut ring [11.0, 8.7, 6.8]→[11.5, 11.15, 9.2], `gm`, top `gmHi`. It gives a step where the barrel enters.
- Top: two chrome edge lines (`silverMid`, z 6.62–6.95 and 9.05–9.38, height 11.43) and a dark rib groove down the
  middle (z 7.9–8.1, height 11.41). All of them are texture-thin and inside the sight corridor's limits (section 5).
- **Side plate, both sides**, in four pieces around the port window plus a stepped rear corner (z 9.4–9.47, outline
  off, so the dark core is the frame): top [11.7..18.0, 10.5..11.15] with the chrome gradient, bottom
  [11.7..18.0, 7.3..9.0], front [11.7..13.25, 9.0..10.5], rear [16.95..18.0, 9.0..10.5], corner [18.0..18.6, 7.3..10.2].
  The window [13.25..16.95, 9.0..10.5] shows the core's dark side.
- **Pins** both sides: [15.5..16.1] and [17.25..17.85] × [7.85..8.45], 0.08 proud, `silverDark` with `gmDark` ink. At
  density 4 that is 3×3 texels, a screw head.
- Roll mark both sides: two thin `silverDark` lines on the bottom piece, [13.2..15.0, 8.1..8.2] and
  [13.2..14.4, 8.35..8.45]. Lines only, never letters or a brand.
- Loading port under the receiver: unchanged ([11.9, 6.95, 7.15]→[14.5, 7.0, 8.85], `port`, ink `gmHi`).
- Rear notch posts: unchanged geometry, colour key `post` (KEEP) instead of `guard`.
- Tang safety (red accent): [19.0, 10.5, 7.65]→[19.35, 10.68, 8.35], `accent`, ink `gmDark`. It sits well under the
  notch's sight rays.

**Trigger group** (`body`): housing [14.3, 6.5, 7.05]→[19.0, 7.0, 8.95] `gmMid`; a chunkier guard, bottom
[14.2, 4.85, 7.4]→[17.4, 5.45, 8.6] (top `gmHi`) and front [14.2, 5.45, 7.4]→[14.8, 6.5, 8.6]; trigger
[15.8, 5.7, 7.75]→[16.3, 6.5, 8.25] rotated 12°, `steel`.

**Grip** (`body`): P0 [17.6, 7.4] unchanged. Segments `seg(P0, 3.3, 1.5, 22°)` and `seg(J1, 2.9, 1.45, 34°)` in `grip`
with `grain`. The butt cap `seg(J2, 0.8, 1.65, 40°)` is `gm` (black butt). Checkered inset panels on both sides of
each segment (`gripDark` with `check`, outline off, 0.35 inset). One grip screw each side (`silverMid`, rotated with
the upper segment). Builder's tweak: overlap the two segments by about 0.4 so the bend doesn't show a seam (the
prototype still shows it).

**Barrel** (`body`)
- [−0.6, 9.05, 6.95]→[11.0, 10.95, 9.05]: sides `gm`, top `silver` with `chrome`, bottom `gmMid`. It is 0.25 lower
  than now, and the receiver step comes from that.
- Chrome stripe both sides [−0.5..10.9, 10.1..10.85] (`silver` + `chrome`, outline off).
- A dim lower streak [−0.4..4.3, 9.3..9.42] in `gmHi`, ahead of the pump only.
- Clamp [3.3, 7.15, 6.8]→[4.1, 11.1, 9.2] (silver ring around both tubes, `chrome`), with a screw each side
  [3.45..3.95, 7.65..8.15].

**Magazine tube** (`body`)
- [−1.0, 7.3, 7.1]→[11.0, 9.05, 8.9] `gm`, with a chrome stripe both sides [−0.9..3.3, 8.35..8.75].
- End cap [−1.6, 7.2, 7.0]→[−1.0, 9.05, 9.0] (top `gmHi`). Its front face has a `silverMid` disc
  [7.65..8.6 × 7.5..8.5] and a `gmDark` dimple.

**Muzzle block** (`body`)
- [−3.0, 8.85, 6.75]→[−0.6, 11.2, 9.25] `gm`, top `gmHi`: 0.2–0.3 proud of the barrel on every side.
- One vent slot each side [−2.6..−1.1, 10.0..10.45] (`port`). Two square slots read as a face, so use one long slot.
- Bore [−3.02, 9.3, 7.25]→[−3.0, 10.7, 8.75] (`bore`, `silverMid` rim). The bore centre moves from y 10.2 to 10.0.
- Bead unchanged ([−2.7, 11.2, 7.55]→[−1.8, 11.9, 8.45]), now with `glow: true` (a fiber-optic dot; no outline, as
  before). It is the one glowing accent.

**Pump** (part `pump`, moves): body [5.0, 6.55, 6.6]→[8.6, 9.0, 9.4] (`wood`, top `woodLight`, bottom `woodDark`,
`groove` on the sides and top). At density 4 that puts a groove every 0.75 u, five ribs as in the reference. Also:

- A lengthwise `woodDeep` groove both sides [5.1..8.5, 7.65..7.9].
- `woodDark` end collars [4.6..5.0] and [8.6..9.0], 0.1 proud (y 6.45..9.05, z 6.5..9.5).
- Action bars [9.0..12.2, 7.75..8.3] at z 6.95–7.25 and 8.75–9.05 (`gmDark`), into the receiver.
- **The bolt**, both sides, sits on the core's side under the plate's surface: [13.35, 9.1, 9.4]→[15.45, 10.4, 9.46]
  (`silver`, top `silverHi`, ink `silverDark`), with a dark extractor line. At rest it closes the front of the window.
  Racked (+2.4), the front of the window opens dark where the shell flies out, and the rest of the bolt slides *under*
  the plate. A 2.1-long bolt is only possible this way: a bolt drawn over a drawn-on recess must stay inside it.

**Shell**: unchanged boxes and a primer dot on the base [16.0, 9.6, 9.0]→[16.03, 10.0, 9.4] (`silverMid`).

**Flash**: unchanged, moved down 0.2 to the new bore centre (y 10.0).

Optional, not in the prototype: an inset `gmMid` panel with a `gmHi` edge on the receiver's rear face. The aim view
is a dark slab (`proto-out\ads.png`), and this would break it up.

## 4. What our model gets wrong or lacks (vs the current reference)

See `work\r90\annot-ours-gui.png` and `compare-icon-ref-vs-ours.png`.

1. **Colour scheme.** All-bright silver tubes and receiver. The current PG3D gun has gunmetal tubes and frame with
   chrome tops and stripes and only the receiver side plate silver. Ours is the old 9.x look.
2. **Wood.** Orange-brown instead of dark walnut.
3. **Pump position and size.** Ours is at the front and 6.6 u long, like the old model. The current pump is about
   4.4 u, just ahead of the receiver, with a long bare front, a silver clamp and the muzzle block.
4. **Muzzle.** Both tubes end flat at −3 with a thin band. The reference has a taller black muzzle block, a shorter
   tube with an end cap and the notch between them. Our band (top 11.4 right behind the bead) also crosses the sight
   corridor (`checks.js`: 11.4 > 11.206) and clips the bead's lower part.
5. **Receiver.** A plain silver box. It lacks the dark frame and top, the inset side plate, the black-framed window
   with a real bolt, the pins (one on the icon side), the stepped rear and the receiver-over-barrel step.
6. **Grip.** Little rake (18°/30° vs ~34°), plain grain, a wood butt instead of black, no checkering or screw.
7. **Guard.** Thin. The reference's is chunky and black.
8. **Detail density.** About 35 boxes at 2 texels a unit. Any part under ~1 u is all outline. There are no panel
   lines, screws, streaks, vents or edge highlights.
9. **First person.** The receiver's top face is a bright white slab in the corner. In the reference the top is dark,
   and the bright line runs along the barrel's top toward the bead (the prototype does this).

## 5. Hard constraints the rebuild must keep

**Interface**
- `module.exports = ({ pg, aim, display }) => ({ 14, 1014, 2014, 3014 })`, exactly these four numbers. make-item-art
  stops otherwise (GUNS.r90), and wm-ammo checks the built feather.json.
- Gun name `gun_r90` (models `donating:item/gun_r90_*`, texture `gun_r90_tex`; paintSkin and the gunskin item
  definitions `donating:gunskin_r90_<look>` map from these names).

**Parts and frames**
- Parts: `body`, `pump`, `shell`, `flash`, with `hidden: ['flash', 'shell']` and `noFull: ['flash', 'shell']`.
- What the frames move:
  - `gun`: the whole gun, kicked about GRIP and canted about AXIS for the reload.
  - `pump`: `rig({ t: [TRAVEL × pump, 0, 0] })`. Everything that rides with the pump lives in this part: wood, collars,
    action bars, bolt.
  - `shell`: shown 0.44 < p < 0.8 on a shot and p < 0.95 on a reload, moved about SHELL.
  - `flash`: shown p < 0.15 on a fired shot.
- Pivots: GRIP [17.6, 6.5, 8] (keep; the hand), AXIS [12, 9.4, 8], SHELL [14.7, 9.8, 9.2].
- The reload path offsets from SHELL: LOAD_START [−1.2, −4.4, 0.4], LOAD_PORT [−1.55, −2.6, −1.2], LOAD_IN
  [−4.4, −1.35, −1.2]. They aim at the loading port [11.9..14.5] under the receiver. If the port or the window moves,
  move SHELL, the shell boxes and these offsets with it.
- **TRAVEL** (the rack distance) goes from 2.9 to 2.4. That is what lets the pump sit near the receiver. These must
  hold:
  - pump rear at rest + TRAVEL ≤ the receiver front (9.0 + 2.4 = 11.4 ≤ 11.5);
  - the action bars at rest + TRAVEL stay inside the core;
  - the bolt shows in the window both at rest and racked (rest 13.35, racked 15.75–17.85; window 13.25–16.95, with the
    excess under the plate).
- States:
  - `ads: aim({ sightY: 11.9, rearX: 18.8, rearDepth: 1.3, scale: [1.6, 2.4, 2.4] })`
  - `sprint: { rotation: [19.88, −26.19, 4.92], translation: [−6.28, 4.19, 2.95], scale: [0.72 ×3] }`
- Display transforms: keep all six (firstperson, thirdperson, gui, ground, fixed, head) as they are. The prototype's
  extents stay inside the same frame; the butt moved ~0.5 back.

**Aim alignment**
- Sight line y = SY = 11.9 on z = 8. The bead's top is SY at x −2.7..−1.8 (z 7.55–8.45). The rear posts' tops are SY
  at x 18.0–18.8 (z 7.15–7.7 and 8.3–8.85); the notch gap is z 7.7–8.3.
- Nothing else may reach the line except the posts. Within z 7.15–8.85 and x −1.8..18.0, every top must stay under
  the ray from the eye (x = 18.8 + 1.3×16/1.6 = 31.8, y 11.9) to the bead's lower rear (−1.8, 11.2), i.e.
  y(x) = 11.9 − 0.7·(31.8 − x)/33.6. That is 11.61 at x 18, 11.48 at 11.5, 11.32 at 4, 11.22 at −0.6.
- The receiver top stays ≤ 11.4, the muzzle block's top ≤ 11.2, and the clamp ≤ 11.1. `work\r90\checks.js <models dir>`
  tests this. The prototype passes; today's band fails.
- Use KEEP keys for everything aimed through: `bead`, `beadHi`, `post` (rear notch), `bore`, `flash`, `flashCore`.

**Flags and GunFx clocks** (DonatingPhone config `R9_0: { shot: 15, draw: 0, alone: 5, action: 15 }`;
gunfx.js checks this line)
- Flag index 1 only: `worked()` means `on_true: cooldown(15, shot(k, false))`, the pump without kick or flash. Flags 0
  and 2 are unused (no fire flicker, no draw clock).
- The shot clock is 15 ticks. The kick must be spent by tick 5 (p 0.333, where `alone` cuts the clock); the kick ends
  at p 0.35 (≈ 0.002 left).
- The pump must not move before p 0.35. It goes back over p 0.35–0.55 and forward over 0.6–0.85, in step with the
  delayed sounds (back at tick 5, forward at tick 9; R9_0.yml Firearm_Action Open 7 / Close 7, frequency 2).
- Reload: one 4-tick clock per shell (Reload_Duration 4, Ammo_Per_Reload 1, magazine 14), drawn at p 1/8, 3/8, 5/8,
  7/8. The CANTED rest pose holds between shells. Aim flavour k = 0.4, hip k = 1.
- Never add a No_Ammo state (WeaponMechanics puts it over Scope and Sprint).

**Skins** (tools\guns\skins.js; paintSkin throws on a missing key)
- These palette keys must exist: `silverHi`, `silver`, `silverMid`, `silverDark`, `wood`, `woodLight`, `woodDark`,
  `grip`, `gripDark`.
- Patterns on them: Urban `camo` on silver, silverMid, wood. Tiger `stripe` on silver, silverMid. Molten `lava` on
  silver, silverMid, wood, grip (8 frames).
- The new keys (`gm`, `gmHi`, `gmMid`, `gmDark`, `woodDeep`) aren't themed by today's looks, so every skin would keep
  a black barrel. Add them to the three r90 themes in skins.js. Proposed values are in `work\r90\skins-proto.js`, with
  previews in `proto-cmp-skins.png` (top row today, bottom row the prototype):
  - Urban: gm* greys with `camo` on `gm`.
  - Tiger: near-black gm*, cream `woodDeep`.
  - Molten: obsidian gm* with `lava` on `gm`.
- Looks keep their ids (`urban`, `tiger`, `molten`), never renamed or removed. Their appearance changes with the
  remake; the owner should know.
- Patterns are in texels, so at density 4 the tiger stripes, camo blotches and lava cracks come out half the size in
  units. Acceptable in the previews; kit fix in section 6.
- Never recolor the KEEP keys (pg.js refuses).

**Kit limits**
- Every box and rotation origin within −16..32 (the flash reaches −8.6).
- Element rotations stay within ±45° (the grip uses 22/34/40, the trigger 12, the flash ±45).
- A glow box has no outline and is unshaded.

**Do not touch**: R9_0.yml, the GunFx config, the item numbers, the shop icons (`wpn::R9_0::model` 14).

## 6. Kit needs (pg.js, shared by all nine)

None are required: the prototype uses only the existing kit (density, per-face `c`/`pat`, `dirs`, `ink`,
`outline: false`, `glow`, `rot`). Useful additions:
- Patterns that get the density in `ctx` (pgGun passes no ctx today; paintSkin passes frame, frames and theme), so
  skin patterns keep their size in units at density 4.
- Per-edge outline control, e.g. `outline: { left, right, top, bottom }`, for seamless multi-piece plates. The
  prototype works around it with outline-free pieces over a dark core.
- A `bevel` pattern (a light top row and a dark bottom row inside the outline) and a row-groove pattern (horizontal
  bands for grips and finger grooves). `groove` and `serr` only make columns.

## 7. Open points for the coordinator

1. **Which look:** current dark-and-chrome (recommended) or the old all-silver one (cautious). This is the owner's
   call. The current one is what PG3D players know today.
2. Accents beyond the reference: the glowing fiber-optic bead and the red tang safety. Both are small and help the
   "PG3D art" ask. Drop either if the owner wants it strictly realistic.
3. TRAVEL 2.9 → 2.4. It reads fine in `proto-out\pump.png`; the in-game feel is a playtest item.
4. The skins' new theme keys (above) and the finer pattern scale at density 4.
