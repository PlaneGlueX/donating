Target: PG3D's AK-48 (current "Up2" look) at the Photon Shotgun's level of detail, built with tools\guns\pg.js.

## 1. The reference, part by part

Positions are in our units after mapping the icon onto our length (muzzle tip -7.4, butt 27.6; see
`annot-ref-parts.png`). "Ref" = where PG3D has it; "keep/move" = what the proposal does (section 6).

1. **Front sight + muzzle** (ref x -7.5..-4.4): the AK front-sight block grown into a stair: from the barrel top it
   rises in **three steps toward the muzzle** to a tall front post at the very front, whose rear face carries a light
   grey glint panel (#9aa9b7). The muzzle end face is black with a square dark bore. Ref top ≈ y 10.5-11 (as high as
   the scope). Ours: a low slanted compensator (top 8.9).
2. **Barrel bar** (ref x ≈ -4.6..3.5): not a thin barrel but a **thick square bar** (barrel + gas tube as one block),
   about 2.0-2.3 tall and ~2 wide, black, with a band along each side at mid height made of **alternating red and
   grey segments** (from the handguard forward: red, grey, red, grey, red, short grey; each ~1.2-1.4 long); the bottom
   face shows the same stripes darker. Ours: a 0.8 chrome rod.
3. **Handguard** (ref x ≈ 3..9): a black box as tall as the receiver, with a **grid of small grey vent squares** on
   its side (2 rows x 3) and a raised top cover; grey metal bands at both ends. No red/white bands (those are on the
   barrel and the GL). Ours: the handguard wears the white/red bands, no vents.
4. **Grenade launcher** (ref x ≈ -2.4..8.8 under the barrel and handguard): **big**, about 3 tall and as wide as the
   receiver or wider (ours is 1.9 tall). A black **front cap** (~2 long) whose front face has a square bore with a
   grey frame; behind it a grey tube with bands: from the cap back: grey, red, grey, red, grey, white, grey, white
   (two thin red bands close together near the front, a white band further back), a darker line along the bottom;
   then a grey breech, and a grey **trigger housing with a small guard loop** right in front of the magazine.
5. **Laser**: the beam leaves the **front top of the GL** (fp: its top-front-left corner, under the barrel) and runs
   forward. Ours: a box on the handguard's left.
6. **Scope** (ref sits ~2 units further back than ours; keep ours): a boxy black ACOG on a black mount: a bigger
   front box with a **blue objective lens** (#165884 / #1a6796, glint #3a8fd0), a **bright red bar** along its side
   (#d93838 with #750907 under it), a black turret block on top, and in first person a **big blue ocular** at the rear.
   Light grey glints on the top edges.
7. **Receiver** (ref x ≈ 9.2..19.6 in our layout): black, chunky, with: a light grey **top strip** (dust cover) with
   a **white glint block** on its rear half; a long **red stripe** along the side at about 60 % height running from the
   stock to the magazine area (bright #e81a14 with dark #760806 specks); the front third a slightly lighter **side
   plate** with **2 black rectangular vents** (in fp a 2x2 checker of dark squares); steel-light bevel lines on every
   top edge (in fp these read as two parallel light strips along the top).
8. **Magazine**: long banana mag, black with **white and grey ribs** (each rib: a white top line, grey face, black
   gap), stepped front edge; curves forward; it sits **right in front of the grip** (gap ≈ 0.5-1 unit at the top).
   Longer than ours (reaches ≈ y -2.5..-3).
9. **Grip**: black, **4 grey horizontal ribs**, slightly stepped back edge, raked back (≈ 20°), about 60 % of the
   mag's length. Ours: a light-grey checkered grip.
10. **Stock** (ref x ≈ 20..27.6): "black skeleton stock": a **solid-looking black slab** (top line continues the
    receiver, bottom slants down to a tall butt), with a **grey inset frame** inside (grey ring, black centre: reads as
    a skeleton web), a slanted/stepped butt. No red. Ours: an open wire stock (two bars) with red stripes.

Proportions (ref, as fractions of the 35-unit length): stock ≈ 16-20 %, receiver ≈ 30 %, handguard ≈ 17 %, exposed
barrel bar + front ≈ 33 %. Our layout already has the right total; the proposal shortens our handguard (8.6 -> 5.8)
and gives the freed length to the thick barrel bar, and moves the magazine back next to the grip.

## 3. Details to add (where)

Texture recipe for every face (the upgraded kit should do this; density **4 texels per unit**, so a 1-texel outline
is 0.25 unit): dark ink outline; inside it a 1-texel **bevel**: top row and left column +30 %, bottom row and right
column -20 %; faces longer than ~4 units get **panel seams** every 2.5-3.5 units (a dark texel line + a light one
after it); top faces of the receiver rail, handguard and barrel bar get **two parallel light lines** along their
length (the fp look); black metal stays smooth (no speckle, or ≤ 3 % +15 % specks). Stripes, ribs and rivets that are
under ~0.75 unit need `outline: false` (or a light ink), or the outline eats them.

Geometry details (coordinates in section 6):
- **Front sight stair** (F1-F3): 3 steps up to a 4.0-tall post at x -7.0..-6.2, glint panel on the post's back.
- **Muzzle**: square bore on the end face (west), grey frame from the ink.
- **Barrel bar stripes**: 6 proud segments per side (+ the bottom), alternating red / gmL; the red ones with a redB
  top texel row.
- **Handguard**: 2x3 vent squares per side (0.7 x 0.6, `bore` color, `gm` ink = the grey rim), front and rear steel
  bands (proud 0.1), a raised top cover (H5) with 3 dark slots per side.
- **GL**: black front cap with the bore (dark square, grey frame), red/white bands as proud rings, a dark bottom
  strip, the steel breech with a gmL latch button, the trigger housing with a guard loop and a gmL trigger, a steel
  mount block between GL and barrel with 2 gmL screw heads.
- **Laser**: a black box on the GL's front top, left side, with a glowing redB emitter face and the beam.
- **Receiver**: steel top rail with a light top line; a rear latch step (R3) with a white glint on top; the red side
  stripe (both sides; on the right only behind the ejection port); a white glint bar above the stripe on the rear
  half; the front vent plate (gmD) with 2x2 vents; rivets (gmL 0.4 squares) at the trunnions; ejection port and
  selector lever on the right as now.
- **Scope**: the red side bars made thicker (0.7 tall) with a redB top row; the top turret black with a redB top face
  (was all red); steel clamp blocks gripping the rail (2), with gmL bolt heads; a **blue rim** on the eyepiece's rear
  faces (east faces of its 4 walls, key lensB) for PG3D's blue ocular, the hole stays open.
- **Charging handle** (bolt part): a chunkier knob (1.0 x 1.1 x 0.7) on a gmL stem.
- **Magazine**: 4 curved segments (one more than now) of black with proud white + grey ribs on both sides, a magG
  spine on the front face, a thicker black floor plate.
- **Grip**: 4 gripP ribs per side, 3 small palm steps on the back, steel bottom cap.
- **Stock**: front hinge block with a big gmL pin, top rail, slanted bottom rail, a recessed gmD web with a black
  centre window (grey ring = PG3D's skeleton inset), the butt with a stepped top-rear corner and a steel grooved pad.
- **Glow**: only the lens, the red dot, the laser emitter and beam (PG3D's AK has no other lit parts; the red stripes
  are paint). **Decals**: none (no text on the gun).
- **Silhouette steps** added: the front-sight stair, the barrel bar under the handguard top, the handguard's top
  cover, the GL cap stepping below the tube, the receiver's rear latch step, the stock's butt step, grip palm steps,
  the mag's curve and ribs.

## 4. What our model gets wrong or lacks

1. Front: a thin 0.8 chrome barrel and a low compensator; PG3D has a thick striped bar and a tall stepped front sight.
2. The red/white bands are on the handguard; PG3D has them on the barrel (red/grey) and the GL (red/grey/white).
   The handguard lacks its vents.
3. GL too thin (1.9 vs ~3), no black front cap or bore, no trigger housing in front of the mag.
4. Receiver flat: no red stripe, one big grey side plate instead of the vent plate + glint, no bevels or rivets.
5. Magazine 3.4 units ahead of the grip (PG3D: right in front of it), too short, and grey/white blocks instead of a
   black mag with light ribs.
6. Grip light grey and checkered (PG3D: black with grey ribs).
7. Stock: open two-bar wire stock with red stripes (PG3D: solid-looking slab with a grey inset frame, no red).
8. Scope: fine as geometry; its red stripes are thin lines, the turret is solid red, no blue ocular, no mount clamps.
9. Shading: every face is one flat tone with an outline (density 2); PG3D has bevel highlights on every edge, which
   is most of what "PG3D-looking" means here.
10. Parts are thin overall (receiver 2.2 wide, barrel 0.8, mag 1.4): the gun reads spindly in the icon and on cops.
11. (Computed, not checked in the client) the current compensator's top (y 8.9 at x -6.2..-4.4) is above the ADS
    limit (section 5.4), so in the client, where the lens has no back face, it should show as a dark lump at the
    bottom of the scope view; render-item doesn't cull back faces and draws the lens solid blue, so the renders
    can't show it.

## 5. Hard constraints for the rebuild

### 5.1 Module contract
- Returns exactly `{ 5, 1005, 2005, 3005 }` (make-item-art.js GUNS.ak47 = [5, 1005, 2005, 3005]):
  - 5: `byContext('', drawn)`, drawn = `cooldown('', 30, draw)` with `fallback = firing('', FIRE(1))`
  - 1005: `byContext('ads', firing('ads', FIRE(0.4, 'flash2'), rest))`
  - 2005: `byContext('sprint', composite('sprint'))`
  - 3005: `byContext('', cooldown('', 57, reload))`
- Model name `gun_ak47` (skins, gunskin children and tests key on it: `donating:item/gun_ak47_*`,
  `gunskin_ak47_<look>`).
- Every box (and rotation origin) inside -16..32 (the beam ends at -12.6, the flash at -13.4).

### 5.2 Parts the frames move
- `body` (static), `bolt` (charging handle: FIRE cycles it +0.6..+2.8 x at hip; reload pulls it +2.8 x at
  p 0.725-0.79), `mag` (reload: rocks -25° about MAG_PIVOT, drops 15 along its own axis, hidden 0.32 < p < 0.4),
  `flash`, `flash2` (`hidden` and `noFull`: only shown by FIRE poses; ADS shows only flash2).
- New (proposed): **`fore`**, the parts above the ADS limit (front-sight stair, muzzle lip, handguard top cover).
  In `_full` and every non-ADS pose; **hidden in every ADS pose and the ADS rest pose**: FIRE(k < 1) poses get
  `hide: ['fore']`, and `firing('ads', ..., { hide: ['fore'] })`. If the builder prefers not to add a part, every
  box ahead of the scope must stay under the ADS limit instead (front post ≤ 8.25 at the muzzle).
- Pivots (model units): SHOULDER [17.6, 5.4, 8] (kick, draw); reload gun pivot [12, 6, 8] (may move to [13.4, 6, 8]
  with the mag); MAG_PIVOT = the mag's front-top: now [10.3, 5.0, 8], **[12.7, 5.0, 8] after the move**; GRIP rotation
  {angle 20, z, origin [17.6, 5.0, 8]}; mag segment origins: see 6.9.
- The reload's phase points (sounds in AK_47.yml are timed to them, delayBeforePlay 4 / 33 / 43 = p 0.12 mag out,
  0.57 seated, 0.725 handle): keep `reload(p)`'s timings, or change AK_47.yml's delays with them.

### 5.3 States and display (keep)
- display: firstperson rot [0, -82, 0] tr [-5.26, 4.11, -1.79] s 0.68; thirdperson [0, -90, 0] [0, 1.5, -5.5] 0.8;
  gui [0, 180, -40] [-0.2, 0.6, 0] 0.47; ground [0, 0, -45] [-1, 8.25, 0] 0.9; fixed [0, 0, -45] [-0.5, 1, -1] 0.9.
- ads = `aim({ sightY: 10.2, rearX: 15.4, rearDepth: 0.8, scale: [1.6, 2.4, 2.4] })` (= rot [0, -90, 0],
  tr [-8.96, 3.04, -13.12]); sprint rot [21.5, -25.25, 0] tr [-10.6, 2.84, 1.38] s 0.72.
- Length -7.4 (muzzle tip) .. 27.6 (butt): the display framings and the flash (core x -9.4..-7, star to -13.4,
  centred on y 7.75, z 8) are tuned to it. **The bore stays at y 7.75, z 8, the muzzle tip at x -7.4.**
- What moving things would break: the scope (rearX 15.4, SY 10.2) -> the ADS framing and the ADS limit line, the
  reload's handle pose (BOLTPOSE turns the gun so the handle shows beside the scope); the grip -> SHOULDER, the GRIP
  origin, the fp framing; the length -> every display transform and the flash. The magazine move (6.9) only touches
  the mag's own boxes, MAG_PIVOT, the segment origins and the rock clearance (re-render the reload).

### 5.4 Aim alignment (the scope is the sight)
- The eye in ADS sits at model (23.4, 10.2, 8) (computed from the ads display; scale [1.6, 2.4, 2.4]).
- The sight line y = 10.2, z = 8 passes, from the eye: the eyepiece opening (x 14.6..15.4, inner ±0.75), the body
  tube (10.0..14.6, inner ±0.85), the bell (9.2..10.0, inner ±1.05), the **red dot** (x 9.5..9.7, y 10.05..10.35,
  z 7.85..8.15, glow, key `dot`) with its post below (x 9.55..9.65, y 9.15..10.05, key `bore`), then the lens
  (x 8.7..9.2, **no east face**, so it's see-through from inside in the client) and the lensB glint (west face only).
  Keep all of these boxes exactly (geometry and keys); only their outsides get detail.
- The tube's front inner edge (x 10, ±0.85) limits the view: half-angle 0.0951 (in model y units scaled 2.4/1.6).
  **ADS limit**: anything ahead of the scope with |z - 8| < 0.0634·(23.4 - x) and y > 10.2 - 0.0634·(23.4 - x)
  shows inside the scope view. Limit heights: x -7.4: 8.25; -4.6: 8.42; 0: 8.72; 3.4: 8.93; 5: 9.03; 8.7: 9.27.
  The proposal keeps the barrel bar ≤ 8.4, the handguard ≤ 8.8 (bands) and puts everything taller in `fore`.
  Parts outside the tube view are hidden behind the scope's outer walls in ADS anyway (|angle| < ~0.2).
- Everything inside the ADS view uses KEEP keys: the tube walls `scope`/`scopeT` (inner faces), `dot`, the post
  `bore`, `lens`, `lensB` (incl. the new eyepiece rim). flash2's top is meant to show at the bottom of the view.

### 5.5 Clocks and flags
- GunFx `AK_47: { shot: 0, draw: 30, fire: 5 }`: the draw clock is 30 ticks (`cooldown('', 30, draw)`), the reload
  clock 57 ticks (73 from empty: the same frames stretched), no shot clock, no action clock.
- Flags: only **0** (firing; set per shot, cleared 5 ticks after the last) through `firing()`; the AK uses no flag 1
  or 2. Fire held + flag 0 -> the random flicker (wobble false) over the 4 FIRE poses.

### 5.6 Palette keys (skins)
- tools\guns\skins.js themes for the AK use: **recv, recvT, gm, gmD, gmL, edge, red, redB, white, gl, magG, magW,
  magK, gripP** (crimson, golden, h4ck3r, kingpin; pattern overrides on recv, gm, magG). All 14 must stay in the
  palette (paintSkin throws on a theme key the gun lacks) and must stay the keys of the materials they name, so a
  Golden AK is gold where the gun is black and a Crimson AK keeps its silver bands.
- **No new keys for skinnable materials** unless all four themes get them too: a new `recvD` for black shadow faces
  would stay black on the Golden AK. Prefer tones derived from a base key by the kit (e.g. `{ key: 'recv', f: 0.7 }`)
  or the bevel layer. Note paintSkin replaces a box's pattern with the theme's (h4ck3r's `code` on recv): the bevel and
  outline should be a layer applied after the pattern, or skinned guns lose their bevels.
- KEEP keys the AK uses (never recolored, never on skinnable parts): **scope, scopeT, dot, beam, lens, lensB, bore,
  flash, flashCore**. (pg.js KEEP also lists flash2, scopeIn, glass, glassB, reticle, post, sight, bead, beadHi.)
- Golden's "the scope's tube stays dark" relies on the tube using scope/scopeT: keep that.

### 5.7 Texture size
- Now 256² at density 2 (73 elements, ~1,280 units² of surface). At density 4 with ~170 boxes expect ~30,000
  texels: aim for 256², accept 512². Big single-color glow faces (flash, flash2, beam) can stay at density 1-2 if the
  kit allows a per-box density. The 8-frame H4CK3R skin stacks 8 textures (512² -> 512x4096 in the atlas).
