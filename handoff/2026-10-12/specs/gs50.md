# Classic Pistol (gs50) remake: design spec

- Module: `tools\guns\gs50.js` (WeaponMechanics `50_GS`, item numbers 9 / 1009 / 2009 / 3009).
- Reference: PG3D's starting pistol "Pixel Gun" (fan wiki pictures in `refs\Pixel_Gun_PG3D\`). The design is the
  current Appearance icon (`01-FirstPistol_icon1_big`, the 21.8.0 look). The older looks (02, 03, 04, the gallery
  shot 10, the Checkmate replica 09, the in-use first-person shot 06) back up the shape and the first-person side.
  Nothing here is copied from the game. Every color below was sampled from the wiki pictures.
- **A working prototype of this spec exists**: `work\gs50\proto\gs50-proto.js` (the full box list, palette, density
  and ink). It was built offline with a copy of the kit plus 3 proposed patterns (`work\gs50\proto\pg-proto.js`) and
  rendered with a patched copy of render-item.js that reads `proto\pack`. Nothing in the repo was touched. Treat its
  numbers as the starting point. The renders are listed at the end.
- Note on the reference files: the `.png` / `.jpg` files in `refs\` are really WebP. Windows' WIC decoder drops their
  alpha channel, so transparent pixels come out black. I rendered them with headless Chrome (`work\gs50\shot.sh`) instead.

Axes, as in every gun module: 1 unit = 1/16 block. The gun runs along x with the muzzle toward -x, y is up, and the
gun is centered on z = 8. **North (z < 8) is the gun's right side.** That side is what the gui/shop icon, the ground,
item frames and third person show, and the reference icon shows it too. **South (z > 8) is the left side.** That is
what the player sees in first person, together with the rear face (east, +x) and the top. So the south side and the
rear face carry the gun on screen almost all the time.

---

## 1. What the reference looks like, part by part

The reference is a chunky P99-style pistol: a tall chrome slide on top of a dark steel frame, a chunky rail block under
the front, a chrome trigger guard, and a raked blood-red grip with a charcoal base plate. It has thick charcoal outlines
everywhere. The parts below are numbered as in `annot-ref01-parts.png`. Positions are given in our units, mapped from
the reference with the slide side's rear at x 15.2 and its front at x 2.8. The icon is a 3/4 perspective view, so
lengths are within about ±0.5 unit.

| # | Part | Reference | In our units |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Muzzle cap | A separate block at the slide's nose, proud of the slide by a hair. Its front face is light chrome with a **square** dark bore in the middle and a chrome rim around it. | x 1.8-2.8, y 9.05-12.75, z 6.6-9.4. Bore 1.3 x 1.3, centred y 10.9 |
| 2 | Front sight | A small nub on top at the front | keep x 2.6-3.6, top = SY 13.2 |
| 3 | Rear sight | A nub at the top rear (in first person, a dark notch block across the rear) | keep the two posts x 13.4-14.6, top SY + 0.3 |
| 4 | Front slide panel | The front ~40% of the slide side, framed by a thick black line, with 3 chrome bands top to bottom (white, light blue, mid blue) | right side x 3.1-7.4 |
| 5 | Ejection port | A black hook-shaped cut at the top edge mid-slide, with the chrome barrel hood showing in it (silver-white in the Halloween skin, `ref11-front-x5.png`) | x 7.6-10.4 (right side only), wraps onto the top to z 7.7 |
| 6 | Rear section | The rear ~35% of the slide side: 6 vertical serration ridges, light and blue alternating | x 10.8-14.8, both sides |

| 9 | Dust cover / rail | A chunky dark navy block under the front half, about half the slide's height, with a row of dark rectangular slots on its side and teeth underneath | x 2.9-7.6, y 6.9-8.6, plus lugs to y 6.55 |
| 10 | Trigger guard | A squared, chunky **chrome** guard (light blue with a dark opening), in front of the grip | front bar x 6.5-7.4, bottom bar y 4.7-5.6, front hook |
| 11 | Grip side | Blood red. A lighter panel with a vertical orange highlight streak and 2 dark screw specks (upper middle, lower rear). The back edge **steps out** going down (a palm swell) | x 10.2-14.1 (upper) / 10.2-14.5 (lower), rake 15° |
| 12 | Front strap | Darker maroon (the reference shows it as a separate face because of the 3/4 view), with a small brown inset | the grip's west and east faces |
| 13 | Base plate | Charcoal, slightly wider than the grip | x 10.0-14.7, y 0.2-1.0, rotated with the grip |

Proportions checked against the reference (normalized to our slide length of 13.2):
- Slide height to length is about 1 : 3.9 in both. Ours is already right (3.4 x 13.2).
- Width: the cap face looks nearly square, but that comes from the perspective. Keep the slide at 2.5 wide and get the
  chunkiness from the cap (2.8 wide), the overlays and the steps.
- The dust cover is about 0.5x the slide height (1.7 units). Ours had only a 0.9 sliver of rail.
- Grip: length ≈ 0.55 x slide (≈ 7.4 with the base), width ≈ 0.3 x slide (≈ 4-4.3). Ours already matches (4.2-4.6 x 7.4).
  The rake reads as 15-25° in the pictures. Keep 15°.
- First person (06): seen from behind, the slide's left side carries one long outlined panel and the rear face is
  dark with a chrome rim. The grip is long and dark red-brown.

**Keep the overall length, grip and sight positions.** The only change is that the muzzle cap sticks 0.2 further forward
(front face at x 1.8 instead of 2.0). That front face is exactly where the flash's core already ends, so the flash needs
no change. Moving other things would break:
- **Sight line** (y = SY 13.2, z = 8): `aim()` computes the aimed translation from `sightY`. Any change moves the
  crosshair off the front post.
- **Rear sight x** (rearX 14): `aim()` uses it for the eye distance. Move it and you must update `rearX`.
- **Grip position and rake** (15° about [12.3, 7.4, 8]): several things depend on it. The first-person and third-person
  display transforms put the hand there. The shot kick pivots on `GRIP` [12, 6, 8]. The reload drops the magazine
  along the rake (`sin(15°)` in `reload`). The magazine must stay hidden inside the grip at rest.
- **Length**: the first-person framing (display translation and scale), the gui scale, the flash position.

In PG3D the chrome changes **hue** as it darkens: highlights are near-white cyan, the base is a saturated sky blue and
the shade is a deeper blue (R/B ratio 0.97, then 0.87, then 0.72). The kit's multiplicative `chrome` gradient keeps the
hue and only turns it gray, which is why ours reads silver-gray. The proposed `bands` pattern (section 6) reproduces this.

## 3. Details to add (all built in the prototype)

Slide part (moves with the shot's slide pull):
- **Muzzle cap** x 1.8-2.8, y 9.05-12.75, z 6.6-9.4. It is proud of the slide by 0.15, so its own outline draws the
  black joint line. Front face (west) `chromeDark` with the **square bore** (x 1.75-1.8, y 10.25-11.55, z 7.35-8.65,
  `bore`, outline false).
- **Main slide** x 2.8-15.2 (it starts behind the cap), y 9.2-12.6, z 6.75-9.25: `bands`, underside and rear face `chromeDark`.
- **Framed panels** (0.05-thick overlays, one face each, own outline): right side x 3.1-7.4 and left side (first person)
  x 3.1-10.2, both y 9.55-12.25, `bands`. The left side's long panel is the old look's long panel, the one the in-use
  shot shows.
- **Serrations**: overlays x 10.8-14.8, y 9.5-12.3 on both sides, kit pattern `serr`. At density 3 that is 12 texels,
  which gives exactly 6 ridges like the reference.
- **Ejection port** (right side only): a dark overlay x 7.6-10.4, y 11.0-12.6 (0.07 proud) and a dark top strip
  x 7.6-10.4, z 6.75-7.7. Inside it, the **barrel hood**: x 7.9-10.1, y 11.3-12.68, z 6.62-7.55, `chrome` `bands`,
  faces north/up/west/east. This gives the reference's black "hook" with chrome inside.
- **Glowing sight dots** (`dot`, `glow: true`, east face only, 0.05 thick): front x 3.6-3.65, y 12.72-13.02,
  z 7.85-8.15; rear y 12.89-13.09 at z 7.15-7.35 and 8.65-8.85. They are aligned for the aimed view (rear dots centred
  0.2 below the line, front 0.33: the perspective evens them out). In `p-ads-zoom.png` they read as a 3-dot sight, and
  they glow at night (`light_emission` 15). Optional, but recommended: they are this gun's "glowing part". If the rear
  dots feel loud, drop them and keep only the front one.
- **Rear stub** x 15.2-15.7, y 9.9-11.5, z 7.4-8.6 (chrome `bands`) with a dark dot on its east face (x 15.7-15.75,
  y 10.5-10.9, z 7.8-8.2). It replaces the hammer, and it is the first thing the player sees on the rear face.

Body part (fixed):
- **Frame band** x 2.9-14.4, y 8.6-9.2, z 6.85-9.15, `frameLight` `bevel`.
- **Dust cover** x 2.9-7.6, y 6.9-8.6, z 6.9-9.1, `frame`, sides `slots` (a row of dark slots, each with a lit row
  under it), top `frameLight`. Under it, **3 rail lugs** x 3.3-4.1 / 4.7-5.5 / 6.1-6.9, y 6.55-7.0, z 7.15-8.85.
  These are silhouette steps, the PG3D "teeth".
- **Frame** x 7.6-13.4, y 7.4-8.6 (`frame` `bevel`). It must run back to 13.4: with it stopping at 10.6, the prototype
  showed a gap between the grip and the slide.
- **Squared chrome guard**: front x 6.5-7.4, y 5.6-7.4; bottom x 6.3-10.6, y 4.7-5.6 (z 7.1-8.9, `bands`). A front
  hook x 5.9-6.3, y 4.7-5.3 (`chromeDark`). The bottom bar reaches back to the raked front strap.
- **Trigger** (unchanged), **slide stop lever** on the left side x 8.6-11.4, y 8.62-9.02, z 9.15-9.3 (`steel`: seen in
  first person), **takedown pin** on both sides x 9.2-9.87, y 7.75-8.42 (`chrome` with `ink: 'chromeDark'`: a mid-blue
  2x2 pin head).
- **Tang** x 13.4-15.1, y 7.6-8.6 (`frame` `bevel`). It closes the space under the slide's rear, where the hammer was.
  The in-use shot shows a grey block there.
- **Grip** (rot 15° about [12.3, 7.4, 8], all of it):
  - Upper x 10.2-14.1, y 4.4-7.6; lower x 10.2-14.5, y 1.0-4.4; both z 6.75-9.25. The back steps out 0.4 going down
    (palm swell).
  - Sides `leather` `check`; front strap (west) `leatherDark` with `groove` (finger grooves); back strap (east) `leatherDark`.
  - **Panels** both sides x 10.6-13.7, y 1.5-6.9 (`leather` `speck`, outlined).
  - **Orange streak** x 12.3-12.8, y 2.1-6.3 (`leatherHi`, outline false: at 2 texels wide it would be all outline).
  - **2 screws** x 11.4-12.07, y 5.6-6.27 and x 11.0-11.67, y 2.3-2.97 (`bore`: 2x2 dark specks).
  - **Base plate** x 10.0-14.7, y 0.2-1.0, z 6.65-9.35 (`frame` `bevel`, top `frameLight`).
- **Barrel** (optional, cheap) x 1.9-6.0, y 10.1-11.7, z 7.25-8.75 (`chromeDark` `chrome`), with a bore on its west
  face. It is fully hidden inside the cap and slide at rest. When the slide recoils (up to 1.8 back) the front of the
  barrel shows, like a real pistol. It shows for about 2 frames of the 6-tick shot (the flash hides the first ones), so
  it is a nicety.

Mag part (hidden in the grip, drops on reload): body unchanged (x 10.8-13.6, y 1.2-7.0, `steel`). Its base (x 10.6-13.8,
y 0.25-1.2) becomes `frame` with a `frameLight` top, so it matches the grip's base plate. New: a **brass top round**
x 11.4-13.0, y 7.0-7.45, z 7.65-8.35, which stays inside the grip (its top is 7.6), and **4 witness holes**
(`bore`, 0.5 squares at x 12.7-13.2, y 2.2 + 1.2k, north face). Everything uses the grip's rotation.

Flash part: **unchanged** (its core ends at x 1.8, the cap's new front face).

Density and ink: `density: 3`, `ink: [36, 36, 38]`. Density 3 makes our texel the size of the reference's: its slide
side is about 38 x 10 texels, and ours becomes 40 x 11. At density 3 a face under 1 unit (2 texels) is all outline, so
keep details that need an inner color at least 1 unit, or set `outline: false` / `ink`. The prototype's texture is
128 x 128.

## 4. What our model gets wrong or lacks

1. **Chrome is gray and smooth**: (214, 224, 238) with a multiplicative gradient, against PG3D's banded, hue-shifting
   blue chrome. This is the biggest single difference in the icon.
2. **The slide is one flat slab.** It has no framed panel, no muzzle cap step, a flush bore face (`west` 0.1 thick),
   the port is only a thin dark slit with no barrel hood, and the serrations are lines rather than ridges.
3. **No dust cover / rail block.** It has a 0.9-tall serrated sliver instead of a chunky block with slots and teeth.
4. **The trigger guard is a thin dark L** instead of a chunky squared chrome guard.
5. **The grip** is one orange-brick tone (176, 56, 26) with specks. It has no maroon front strap, no panel, no orange
   streak, no screws and no palm-swell step, and its base is brown leather instead of a charcoal plate.
6. **It has an external hammer**, which the reference doesn't have. The rear stub with the striker dot is missing.
7. **The rear face** (in first person all the time) is plain chrome with two posts.
8. **The outlines** are blue-black (16, 18, 24) and 1 texel at density 2, so its texels are about 1.5x coarser than the
   reference's.
9. **The magazine** is a plain box. Nothing shows during the reload drop: no round, no witness holes.
10. **The gui icon** is a flat profile. The reference icon is a 3/4 view showing the muzzle face and the front strap
    (see section 7).

## 5. Hard constraints the rebuild must keep

- **Parts the frames move** (the names are load-bearing): `body` (fixed), `slide` (the shot pulls it back:
  `rig({ t: [1.8 * ease.bump(p, 0, 0.75, 0.12), 0, 0] })`), `mag` (the reload: drop `-10` along the 15° rake, then
  hidden for `p` 0.40-0.46, then back up), `flash` (`hidden` and `noFull`, shown for `p` < 0.34 of a shot). The whole
  gun's `gun` rig: the shot kick pivots on `GRIP = [12, 6, 8]`, the reload roll pivots on [8, 8, 8]. New details must
  go in the right part. Anything on the slide (cap, bore, panels, serrations, port, hood, sights, dots, stub) goes in
  `slide`. Frame, guard, grip and barrel go in `body`. Round and witness holes go in `mag`.
- **States**: `''` (Default), `ads` = `aim({ sightY: 13.2, rearX: 14, rearDepth: 2, scale: [1.6, 2.4, 2.4] })`,
  `sprint` = `{ rotation: [-25, -60, 0], translation: [-6.56, 2.09, -1.96], scale: [0.56, 0.56, 0.56] }`. The display
  block stays as is (firstperson [0, -80, 0] / [-5.35, 4.44, -1.13] / 0.56, thirdperson, gui, ground, fixed, head).
- **Aim alignment**: the sight line is y = 13.2 on z = 8. The front post (x 2.6-3.6, y 12.6-13.2, z 7.6-8.4) has its
  **top face exactly on the line**. The rear posts (x 13.4-14.6, z 6.9-7.6 and 8.4-9.1, top 13.5) frame it, so the
  line passes **through the notch** between them. Nothing may rise into z 7.6-8.4 above about y 12.8 anywhere between
  the rear posts and the eye or the front post. Specifically, **no notch floor**: the prototype tried a dark floor
  x 13.4-14.6, y 12.6-12.8 and it hid the bright slide top under the post, so the post lost its contrast. The floor
  was taken out again, and the bright slide top stays visible. The hood (z ≤ 7.55, y ≤ 12.68), dots (beside the notch) and cap
  (y ≤ 12.75) were checked in `p-ads-zoom.png`.
- **Frames and clocks**: the shot is `cooldown('', 6, shot(1))` and `cooldown('ads', 6, shot(0.55))`, matching GunFx
  `50_GS: { shot: 6, draw: 0 }`. The reload is `cooldown('', 32, reload)`, matching `Reload_Duration: 32` (38 from
  empty: the frames stretch). The reload's key moments are tied to the sounds in `50_GS.yml`: the magazine leaves from
  `p` 0.12 (`mag_out`, delayBeforePlay 3) and is slapped home at `p` 0.73 (`mag_in`, delayBeforePlay 24). Keep these
  `p` values, or change the delays with them.
- **Flags**: none. The Classic Pistol uses no `firing()` (flag 0 is for the automatics) and no `flag(1/2)` (no pump or
  bolt, no draw clock, since draw is 0). Don't add any.
- **Numbers returned**: exactly `9`, `1009`, `2009`, `3009` (make-item-art.js `GUNS.gs50`; the build throws on missing
  or extra numbers). 9 = Default (shot frames), 1009 = Scope (aimed shot frames), 2009 = Sprint (static), 3009 = Reload
  (32 frames). In every state the first person is animated and everything else uses `_full` (`byContext`).
- **Palette keys skins.js uses for gs50** (all 4 looks: safety, bubblegum, glitch, mastermind): `chrome`,
  `chromeDark`, `frame`, `frameLight`, `leather`, `leatherDark`, `steel` (mastermind). The glitch look also sets
  `pat: { chrome: 'glitch', frame: 'glitch' }`. **These 7 keys must stay in the palette**, because paintSkin throws on a
  theme key the gun lacks. They should keep their roles (chrome = the slide's color, leather = the grip's) so the looks
  still read. **New keys need a value in each of the 4 themes**, or they keep the default color on every skin. The
  prototype's values for `leatherHi`, checked in `proto-skins.png`: safety [255, 128, 24], bubblegum [255, 172, 212],
  glitch [40, 240, 255], mastermind [212, 170, 60]. `brass` and `dot` need none. Never rename or remove a look.
- **KEEP keys** (never recolored by skins; pg.js refuses): flash, flashCore, flash2, beam, lens, lensB, bore, scope,
  scopeT, scopeIn, glass, glassB, reticle, dot, post, sight, bead, beadHi. This gun uses `bore`, `post`, `dot`,
  `flash`, `flashCore` (and still lists `sight`). The sights and bore stay dark and the dots stay green on every skin.
- **Kit limits**: every box and rotation origin must be within -16..32 (the prototype is). The magazine must be fully
  inside the grip and base at rest, because it is in `_full` too. Other things that read this gun: the shop icon
  (`wpnIcon` feather 9, i.e. the gui `_full`), cops' and bodyguards' held guns (third person `_full`), core.sk's 4 gun
  skin lines and crates.js (`gunskin_gs50_glitch`). None of them depends on geometry.

## 6. Kit needs (pg.js, shared by all nine)

The prototype adds 3 patterns. Code is in `work\gs50\proto\pg-proto.js`, between "PROPOSED for the remake" and
"A glowing part". None of them calls the gun's random generator, so adding them changes no other gun's texture.
- `bands`: PG3D chrome. A side face gets discrete bands: mix toward white by 0.8L, then by 0.45L, then the base, and a
  darker, more saturated bottom 2 rows (L = the base's luminance, so dark skins such as mastermind get dark-gray
  highlights instead of white). The up face gets the top tone and the down face the bottom tone, with sparse diagonal
  glints. It is hue-safe: the orange, mint and black skins all work.
- `slots`: a row of dark 2-texel slots, each with a lit texel under it (rails, vents).
- `bevel`: a lit top row and a dark bottom row inside the outline (chunky dark blocks).

Without the kit change, `chrome` and `groove` are the fallback, but the chrome then stays gray (section 4.1).

## 7. Open points for the coordinator (cross-gun)

- **Gui angle**: every gun uses a flat `[0, 180, -3x]` profile. The reference icons are 3/4 views. `p-gui-34.png`
  shows this gun at `[15, 145, -30]`, which shows the muzzle face and the maroon front strap, much closer to the wiki
  icon. Decide this for all nine or none.
- **Density**: 3 here. If the style decision for the nine is uniform, use the same value everywhere. At density 2,
  every overlay above should be at least 1.5 units tall to show more than its outline.
- **Glow**: this gun's glowing part is the 3 tritium dots, a functional choice that fits a classic pistol. The
  reference itself has none (only its Halloween skin glows).
